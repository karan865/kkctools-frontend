## 5. Mathematical Calculation Engine & Edge Cases

### 5.1 Unit Conversion Normalization (To Decimal Feet)
All measurements must first be normalized into decimal feet before calculating square footage:
- Inches to Feet: `ft = inches / 12`
- Yards to Feet: `ft = yards × 3`
- Meters to Feet: `ft = meters × 3.28084`
- Centimeters to Feet: `ft = cm / 30.48`

### 5.2 Area Calculation Formulas
1. **Rectangle:**  
   $$\text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} = \text{Length}_{\text{ft}} \times \text{Width}_{\text{ft}}$$
2. **Triangle:**  
   $$\text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} = \frac{\text{Base}_{\text{ft}} \times \text{Height}_{\text{ft}}}{2}$$
3. **Circle:**  
   $$\text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} = \pi \times r_{\text{ft}}^2$$
4. **Trapezoid:**  
   $$\text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} = \frac{a_{\text{ft}} + b_{\text{ft}}}{2} \times h_{\text{ft}}$$

### 5.3 Waste & Final Output Formulas
- **Net Area:** $\text{Net} = \sum \text{Area}_i$
- **Gross Area with Waste:**  
  $$\text{Gross} = \text{Net} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Waste \%}}{100}\right)$$
- **Conversions:**  
  - $\text{Sq Meters} = \text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} \times 0.092903$  
  - $\text{Sq Yards} = \text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} / 9$  
  - $\text{Sq Inches} = \text{Area}_{\text{sqft}} \times 144$
- **Total Cost:**  
  $$\text{Total Cost} = \text{Gross Area} \times \text{Price per Sq Ft}$$

### 5.4 Edge Case Handlers
- If inputs are negative or 0, display clean validation message (`"Please enter a dimension greater than 0"`).
- Mixed Feet & Inches inputs (e.g., 12 feet 6 inches = $12 + \frac{6}{12} = 12.5\text{ ft}$).
- Rounding: Display results rounded to 2 decimal places (e.g., `185.34 sq ft`), but keep raw numbers in memory for exact unit conversion.

### [H2] What Is Square Footage?
**Square footage** (abbreviated as *sq ft* or *ft²*) is a standard imperial unit of measurement used primarily in the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom to measure two-dimensional area. One square foot is defined as the area enclosed within a square whose sides each measure exactly one linear foot (12 inches).

In construction, real estate, and home remodeling, square footage is the universal metric used to:
- Determine property valuations and home listing prices.
- Estimate the cost of flooring materials (hardwood, laminate, vinyl plank, tile, and carpet).
- Calculate paint, primer, and drywall coverage.
- Plan concrete slabs, patios, turf installations, and lawn fertilizer application.

### [H2] How to Calculate Square Feet: Formulas by Shape
When calculating square feet manually, the mathematical formula depends on the geometric shape of the space.

#### 1. Rectangular or Square Spaces
The vast majority of rooms, walls, and patios are rectangles or squares.
$$\text{Square Footage} = \text{Length (feet)} \times \text{Width (feet)}$$
*Example:* A rectangular garage measuring 20 feet long and 12 feet wide:
$$\text{Area} = 20\text{ ft} \times 12\text{ ft} = 240\text{ sq ft}$$

#### 2. How to Measure Square Footage of an L-Shaped Room
Irregular L-shaped living rooms or open floor plans should be split into two separate rectangular boxes (Box A and Box B):
1. Measure Box A (e.g., 10 ft × 12 ft = 120 sq ft).
2. Measure Box B (e.g., 8 ft × 14 ft = 112 sq ft).
3. Sum the two areas: $120 + 112 = 232\text{ sq ft}$.

#### 3. Triangular Areas and Gable Roofs
For triangular spaces or attic gables:
$$\text{Square Footage} = \frac{\text{Base (feet)} \times \text{Height (feet)}}{2}$$
*Example:* A triangular patio with an 18-foot base and a 10-foot height:
$$\text{Area} = \frac{18 \times 10}{2} = 90\text{ sq ft}$$

#### 4. Circular Spaces (Patios, Fire Pits, Pools)
For round areas:
$$\text{Square Footage} = \pi \times \text{Radius}^2 \quad (\pi \approx 3.14159)$$
*Tip:* The radius is half of the total diameter across the circle. For a 14-foot round patio (radius = 7 ft):
$$\text{Area} = 3.14159 \times 7^2 = 3.14159 \times 49 \approx 153.94\text{ sq ft}$$

### [H2] Square Footage Calculator FAQ

#### How do you figure out square footage of a room?
To figure out square footage, measure the length and the width of the room in feet using a standard tape measure. Multiply the length by the width. If the room has alcoves or closets, calculate those rectangles separately and add them to the main room's square footage.

#### How is square footage calculated if measurements are in inches?
If your measurements are taken in inches, multiply Length (inches) × Width (inches) to get total square inches. Then divide that result by 144 (since there are 144 square inches in one square foot). For example, a countertop measuring 96 inches by 36 inches = 3,456 sq inches ÷ 144 = 24 square feet.

#### Why should I add a waste percentage to my square footage?
Building materials such as tiles, wood planks, shingles, and drywall sheets must be cut to fit edges, corners, and doorways. Offcuts often cannot be reused. Adding a 10% waste buffer ensures you do not run out of material mid-installation, and provides extra pieces for future repairs.

#### How do I convert square feet to linear feet?
To convert square feet to linear feet, you must know the width of the material:
$$\text{Linear Feet} = \frac{\text{Square Feet}}{\text{Material Width in Feet}}$$
For example, if you have 100 square feet of flooring made from 6-inch (0.5 ft) wide planks, you need $100 / 0.5 = 200\text{ linear feet}$. Use our companion **[Linear Foot Calculator](/linear-foot-calculator/)** for instant conversions.

#### How do real estate agents calculate house square footage?
In US residential real estate, Gross Living Area (GLA) square footage only includes finished, heated, and air-conditioned above-grade living spaces. Unfinished basements, garages, open decks, and attics with ceiling heights below 7 feet are generally excluded from official listing square footage.
