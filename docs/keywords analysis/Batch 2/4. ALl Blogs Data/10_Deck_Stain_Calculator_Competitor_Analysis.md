## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### #1 — cabotstain.com (30 RD)

- feet/metres, length and width (**whole numbers only**), Calculate. Results hidden in capture; “approximate, see product label”. ~100 words, no coverage rate, no FAQ.
- **Lesson:** ranks #1 on brand authority with almost nothing. Decimal inputs, railings and stairs would already beat it.

### #2 — omnicalculator.com/construction/deck-stain (18 RD) — feature benchmark

- **Calculator:** floor L × W; railing (none / standard / custom area) with length, height, post width, filling (balusters density and width); steps (count, width, run, rise); coats (1); coverage (175 ft²/gal); units cm/m/in/ft; show area per element.
- **Content:** how to use; how to calculate stain; railing maths for posts, rails, rectangular and round balusters.
- **FAQs (4):** how much stain for my deck · for a 20×20 deck · how much does a gallon cover · how much do 5 gallons cover.
- **Errors:** 20×20 = 400 ft² ÷ 175 = **2.29 gal**, page says 2.25; balusters “density” input not in its formulas; steps added to floor may double-count.
- **Lesson:** the **railing detail** (posts + rails + balusters) is the strongest feature in the SERP. Match it and fix the maths.

### #3 — stainandsealsupply.com (4 RD)

- 7 dropdowns only — fence/deck, spray/brush, smooth/rough cut, one/two sides, fence type, stain type (transparent / semi-transparent / semi-solid). **No dimension input at all.** Output: gallons.
- **Lesson:** stain **opacity**, **application method** and **wood texture** are factors users recognise. Add them as coverage modifiers — with real dimensions.

### #4 — pittsburghpaintsandstains.com (1 RD)

- Project type (deck, fence, structure, furniture, siding); feet/metres; **add flat surfaces, railings, steps, vertical surfaces**; doors and windows subtracted; outputs total area + gallons for 1 and 2 coats.
- **Weak points:** coverage rate hidden (“based on coverage range”); a developer note “adjustable in code” visible to users.
- **Lesson:** **“add another surface”** + showing **1 and 2 coats together** are good UX ideas.

### #5 — homewyse.com/services/cost_to_stain_deck.html (16 RD)

- ZIP + sq ft → 4 line items (refinishing, staining labour, job supplies, deep wash option); national $2.96–$6.16/sq ft (May 2026); GC overhead +13–22%; 11 references.
- **Error:** all 4 line items show the same range; totals blank.
- **Lesson:** the only page answering **deck staining cost** — and it's flawed. A clear DIY vs pro cost block wins `deck staining cost calculator` (390).

### #6 — twpstain.com/twp-deck-stain-calculator (5 RD)

- **Calculator:** floor L × W; railing total length × height (≈ 3 ft); steps width × depth × count; product (5 TWP lines); wood condition (new/smooth, average, weathered/porous); coats (1 or 2 wet-on-wet). Fence calculator below (both sides yes/no).
- **Content:** coverage by coat (semi-transparent 150–200 then 200–300 sq ft/gal; semi-solid 150–250 then 250–350); prep options; new-wood tips; wet-on-wet method; product prices in menu.
- **Errors:** two-coat average (100–125 sq ft/gal) not derived and disputed in comments; shelf life stated three different ways; risers not counted.
- **Lesson:** **second coat covers more area than the first** — a real detail nobody else models. 1,160 comments show users have many follow-up questions (FAQ source).

### #7 — olympic.com/stain-calculator (1 RD)

- Hub page linking to 4 project sub-calculators (fences, siding, decks/patios, pergolas). No tool on the page. Mislabelled links.

### #8 — wooddefender.com/stain-calculator/ (0 RD)

- **Lesson:** **wood species** matters for absorption; email gating is a turn-off.

## 5. Patterns

- **Core maths:** area (floor + railings + stairs + vertical) × coats ÷ coverage = gallons. Default coverage seen: 175 ft²/gal (Omni, TWP). Ranges quoted 100–350 ft²/gal depending on coat, opacity and wood.
- **Factors competitors use:** coats · opacity · wood condition · wood species · rough vs smooth · spray vs brush · one or both sides (fence) · first vs second coat coverage.
- **What nobody does:** litres, can-size split (gallons + quarts, or 1 L / 2.5 L / 5 L), cost of stain, prep products quantity, board side edges, deck sections, sealer vs stain.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Full deck model:** several floor sections + railings (posts, rails, balusters) + stairs (treads, risers, stringers) + skirting/vertical — Omni + Pittsburgh combined, done right.
2. **Coverage that responds to real factors:** opacity, wood condition, rough/smooth, application method, first vs second coat — all as **visible, editable** multipliers.
3. **Quantity + cost:** stain cost (price per gallon/litre) + prep products + optional labour → DIY vs pro. Only Homewyse has cost (broken).
4. **Can-size planner:** “Buy 1 × 5 gal + 1 × 1 gal” — or litres (1 L / 2.5 L / 5 L) for UK/EU/AU. Nobody does this.
5. **Metric + litres** output.
6. **Prep quantity:** cleaner / brightener / stripper by area (coverage from labels).
7. **Correct examples** (Omni's 20×20 is wrong) and an engine-generated “stain needed by deck size” table.
8. **Board gaps and edges** option (deck boards have sides; spacing reduces top area) — explained simply.
9. **No email gate, no brand bias.**
10. **Trust:** date, sources (manufacturer TDS), methodology; link to Fence Stain page.
