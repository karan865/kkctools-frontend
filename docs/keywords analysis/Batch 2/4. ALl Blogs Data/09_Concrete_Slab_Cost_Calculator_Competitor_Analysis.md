## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### #1 — calculator.net/concrete-calculator.html (358 RD)

- 5 shapes (slab/footing/wall, round column/footing, circular slab/tube, curb & gutter, stairs), quantity, per-field units incl. metric. Output: volume + weight. **No cost, no waste.** ~300 words, no FAQ.
- **Lesson:** ranks #1 on authority only. Doesn't answer the cost intent at all.

### #2 — spencerconcrete.com (0 RD)

- Inputs: pavement area (SF/SY), thickness, **pavement removal (concrete/asphalt)**, **stone aggregate (tons)**. Output not shown. Margins/crew described but not editable.
- **Lesson:** **removal + base stone** are real line items users expect.

### #3 — homeprojectcalculator.com/concrete-cost-calculator/ (4 RD) — content benchmark

- **Title:** Concrete Cost Calculator – Price Per Yard & Sq Ft · **H1:** Concrete Cost Calculator (2026)
- **Calculator:** project type (patio, driveway, garage, shed, footing), imperial/metric, region (national + 7), L, W, thickness, waste 5/8/10%, price per yd³ (hint $120–$200). Outputs: yd³, material cost, materials $/sq ft, area.
- **Content:** price per yd³ by PSI/mix; short-load fee; materials and installed $/sq ft by thickness; slab cost by size; cost by yards ordered; what affects cost; volume quick reference; regional and city tables; planning checklist.
- **FAQs (8):** how much is a yard · cost per sq ft · slab cost · concrete for 10×10 · includes labour? · why waste · minimum ready-mix order · cement vs concrete.
- **Errors:** Texas patio text contradicts its city table; “adding 2 in ≈ 2 yd³” on a 20×20 is really ≈ 2.47 yd³; 1 yd³ minimum vs 3–5 yd³ short-load in the same answer; region input doesn't change any output; FAQ mentions a quote form that isn't there.
- **Lesson:** best structure (price-per-yard + per-sq-ft + by size + by region). Calculator only does **materials** — we add labour, delivery, pump, base, rebar, finish.

### #4 — readymixusa.com (3 RD)

- Slab / footing / curb → total volume (yd³). Footing width in inches vs slab width in feet (confusing). No cost, no waste, © 2020, staging links in footer.

### #5 — concreteblockcalculator.com/concrete-slab/ (0 RD) — best FAQ

- **Calculator:** rectangle or circle; L × W or diameter; thickness presets 3/4/5/6/8 in; waste 10%; checkboxes: **installed cost estimate (US 2026)** and **rebar estimate (#4 @ 12" OC)**. Outputs: yd³, ft³, bags, weight, cost range, rebar. Print/PDF, copy.
- **Content:** formulas (L × W × T ÷ 324 shortcut), bag yields, waste, cost method; installed $/sq ft with add-ons (gravel base, reinforcement, sealing, stamped); regional multipliers; ready-mix $/yd³ tiers; weight per sq ft; rebar & mesh; cure times; thickness and PSI guides; 10 cited sources (ACI 332, IRC R506, ASTM E1745, PCA, Quikrete, WRI, FRED PPI).
- **FAQs (15):** bags for 10×10 / 12×12 / 20×20 · how thick · 4" vs 6" · cost to pour · rebar or mesh · what PSI · bagged vs ready-mix · how much extra · cost per yd³ · weight per sq ft · formula · cure time · slab vs pad.
- **Errors:** several bag counts rounded down (88 should be 89; 244 → 245); 12×12 high cost $1,730 vs 144 × $12 = $1,728; 20×20×6" shows raw volume but waste-adjusted bags; two different ready-mix ranges on the same page.
- **Lesson:** **15-question FAQ + source list** is the trust model. Our numbers must be engine-generated so rounding never drifts.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Every line item, editable, in one tool.** Nobody models concrete + delivery/short-load + pump + labour + base + rebar/mesh + vapor barrier + finish + demolition + permit + overhead together.
2. **Three answers side by side:** DIY bags · DIY ready-mix · contractor installed. Only SlabCalc does a partial version.
3. **Cost per yd³ *and* per sq ft output** (both keyword groups) — nobody outputs installed cost per yd³.
4. **Short-load logic:** under the supplier minimum → automatic short-load fee line; truck count — answers the 10-yard-truck questions.
5. **Currency + metric:** £/€/A$ and m³/m² (10,100 global vol). 0 of 9 offer it.
6. **Region as a simple editable multiplier**, shown, not hidden — and it must actually change the result (HomeProjectCalc's doesn't).
7. **Engine-generated tables** (cost by size, by thickness) — 4 competitors have arithmetic/rounding errors.
8. **Several slabs / shapes in one job** — reuse Concrete Calculator's job list.
9. **Remove-and-replace mode** (only CostFlowAI).
10. **Trust:** dated price basis, cited sources, “how we estimate” section; no fake reviewer (AGENTS rule 3).
