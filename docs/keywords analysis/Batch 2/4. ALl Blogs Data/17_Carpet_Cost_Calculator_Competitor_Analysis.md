## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### #1 — homedepot.com cost-install-carpet (AS 99)

- **H1:** Carpet Installation Cost Guide — no calculator. Carpet $1–$4/sq ft, pad $1.01/sq ft, basic install $0.69/sq ft (promo). Types (pattern, texture/twist, loop/Berber); features (stain, wear, moisture resistance, fibre). Removal and furniture moving mentioned but unpriced. No FAQ, no date.
- **Lesson:** users want **carpet + pad + install** split — the three numbers they see in stores.

### #2 — floorcw.com cost of carpet (AS 9) — content benchmark

- Sections: cost per sq ft; priced by foot/yard/roll; cost to carpet a room, stairs, whole house; installation (what the labour line covers); replacement extras (removal, disposal, subfloor); pad + warranty; by fibre; by style; why carpet costs what it does; ways to save (remnants, roll ends, package deals); **how to estimate before a quote (6 steps)**; carpet vs vinyl plank, laminate, hardwood.
- Worked example: 12×14 bedroom → $1,152 before tax. Explains **why you buy more than the room measures** (roll width).
- Sources: HomeAdvisor (Jun 2026), Fixr, Homewyse, Carpet and Rug Institute, Shaw, Mohawk warranty, state tax guides.
- **FAQs (7):** carpet three bedrooms · 10×12 room · per square metre · carpet tiles & commercial · area rug from carpet · adds value before selling? · best time to buy.
- **Errors:** stairs priced two different ways ($430–$780 vs flat $75–$250 for ~12 steps); source ranges placed side-by-side without reconciling.
- **Lesson:** AS 9 at #2 proves **depth + cited sources** work here. Our calculator turns its 6-step manual method into a tool.

## 5. Patterns

**Line items users expect:** carpet (per sq ft or sq yd, by grade/fibre) · pad · installation labour · old carpet + pad removal · disposal · furniture moving · stairs (per step) · tack strip/transitions · subfloor repair · sales tax.

**Quantity logic (standard trade practice — explain in our words):** carpet comes in 12 ft wide rolls (some 13'6" or 15'); a room wider than the roll needs a seam and extra length; buyers pay for the rectangle cut from the roll, not the room's exact area → typical overage 10–20%. 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft; 1 m² = 10.764 sq ft.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Roll-width-aware quantity:** rooms L × W → seams and roll overage → square yards to buy. 0 competitors calculate it (only FloorCW explains it).
2. **Rooms + stairs + closets + hallway** summed for a whole-house estimate.
3. **Carpet + pad + labour + removal + disposal + furniture + transitions + tax** — every item editable.
4. **Price in sq ft, sq yd and m²** (people see per-yard prices in stores).
5. **Grade/fibre presets** (builder / mid / premium; nylon, polyester, triexta, wool) with sourced ranges.
6. **Replacement mode** — highest CPC term ($8.09).
7. **Carpet vs LVP vs laminate vs hardwood** comparison for the same rooms (link our Laminate and Floor Refinishing tools).
8. **Engine-generated room-size table** (10×10 … 15×20) — no contradictions like FloorCW/InchCalc.
9. **Currency + metric** (UK/AU) — 0 competitors.
10. **Trust:** date, cited sources, methodology.
