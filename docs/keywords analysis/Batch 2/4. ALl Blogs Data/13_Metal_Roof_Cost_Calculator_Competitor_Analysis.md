## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### #1 — roofingcalculator.com/metal-roof-cost (AS 39)

- Address → roof area/pitch from **Google Project Sunroof LiDAR**; material (shingles, tile, metal, fibreglass, fibre cement) + sub-type (aluminium, copper, corrugated, ribbed, steel…); new gutters; tear-off 1/2 layers; stories; skylights; ridge vents; dormers. Cost data from Craftsman, city-adjusted. **No price shown on the page.**
- **Lesson:** stories, skylights, dormers, gutters are inputs homeowners understand. Address/LiDAR needs APIs (not allowed for us).

### #2 — pro.houzz.com metal-roof-cost (AS 74)

- Roof area; profile (standing seam, corrugated, ribbed, metal shingles, metal tiles); metal (galvalume, aluminium, copper, zinc); pitch (low/moderate/steep); region (low/mid/high); tear-off. Outputs promised: cost range, lifespan. ~150 words; no matrix despite the title.
- **Lesson:** **profile × metal** matrix is the right input model.

### #3 — truemetalsupply.com/calculator (AS 26)

- Supplier tool (not captured — JS). Linked: roof & siding visualiser, comparison table, post-frame builder. Mismatched phone numbers, placeholder text.

### #4 — roofingcostcalculator.com metal-roof-costs (AS 12)

- Calculator: project type, 8 materials, slope, tear-off, roof size (26 bands), location size, contractor type, ZIP. Content: metal $8–$30/sq ft; aluminium, copper, steel, tin, zinc ranges; one Seattle quote pair (shingle $19k vs metal $38k).
- **Errors:** headline range starts at $8 but steel starts at $5; empty “average installed cost” column; unsourced lifespans.
- **Lesson:** **contractor size** and **location type** are interesting price modifiers.

### #5 — homewyse.com seam metal roof (AS 47)

- ZIP + sq ft → standing seam material, labour, supplies, equipment, tear-off; national $17.74–$23.95/sq ft (May 2026) for 24-gauge 1" field-lock; 11 references.
- **Error:** five line items with identical ranges; totals blank.
- Note how far its standing-seam price is from others' ($10–16) → pricing basis must be clear.

### #6 — omnicalculator.com/construction/metal-roof-cost (AS 84)

- Roof accessible? yes → L × W; no → footprint + pitch. Panel length/width → **panel count**. Panel price → total. FAQs (5): cost for a house · how to calculate · how much · cost to paint a metal roof · 16×80 mobile home.
- **Errors/weaknesses:** 16"×16" panels at $3 unrealistic; only panel cost (no labour) while FAQ ranges “include installation”; mobile home figure below its own range; no waste.

### #7 — buildvisionai.com metal-roof-cost-calculator (AS 10)

- Inputs: imperial/metric; roof area; material (steel, aluminium, copper, zinc); panel type (standing seam, corrugated, shingles, tiles, R-panel); pitch (degrees); US region; tear-off.
- Content: cost by type; **metal vs asphalt true cost over 30 and 50 years**; pricing factors (material, profile, coating Kynar/PVDF, pitch, tear-off vs overlay, labour); panel-system price per square; formulas (panels, trim, underlayment, fasteners, waste); productivity rates.
- **FAQs (12):** cost · cheaper than shingles? · lifespan · home value · cheapest type · standing seam cost · 2026 cost · standing seam vs corrugated · best gauge · payback vs asphalt · over existing shingles · actual service life.
- **Errors:** standing seam steel priced three different ways ($7–12, $10–16, $10–18); several totals don't match its own $/sq ft; thermal expansion figure overstated.
- **Lesson:** strongest **content model** (lifecycle comparison, coatings, gauge). Its own numbers contradict each other — ours must be engine-generated.

### #8 — costflowai.com/calculators/roofing/ (AS 21)

- Roof replacement calculator (all coverings incl. standing seam $550/sq and corrugated $300/sq). Quick mode (L, W, pitch 2/12–12/12, material, ZIP) and pro mode (22 inputs). Outputs: typical, range, $/sq ft, $/square, squares, bundles, DIY, line items.
- Pitch factor √(1 + (p/12)²); 15% waste on materials; BLS roofer wage cited; covering comparison table; metro multipliers.
- **Errors:** FAQ cost-per-year figures don't match its stated rates; “no overhead included” vs a line labelled “overhead”.
- **Lesson:** **one roof, every covering compared** is what “metal vs shingle” searchers want. (Same page also ranks for Roof Replacement #19.)

### #9 — allroofingcalculators.com metal-roof-calculator (AS 9) — **the page to beat**

- **Calculator:** footprint L × W; pitch 1:12–12:12 (shows factor + angle); roof type (gable, hip, shed, gambrel, mansard); panel type with coverage width (standing seam 16", corrugated 26", R-panel 36", metal shingle 14", stone-coated 22"); material (7 with per-square ranges); eave overhang (12"); waste by roof type (10/15/18/20%). Outputs: sq ft to order, squares, footprint, pitch factor, actual area, **panels needed, panel length**, waste, installed cost, step-by-step breakdown, diagram. Warnings for low pitch and fall protection.
- **Content:** what it measures; inputs; panel types; gauge; step-by-step area; pitch factor table; hip roof; pole barn; panel length; full material estimate; cost per square/sq ft; what changes an estimate; underlayment; waste; minimum pitch; common mistakes; how the calculator was built (NRCA, IRC R905, ASTM D226, OSHA).
- **FAQs (12):** what is it · square footage · cost per square · cost per sq ft · accurate estimate · minimum pitch · lifespan · worth it vs shingles · 1,500 sq ft house at 6:12 · hip roof · over existing shingles · standing seam vs exposed fastener.
- **Errors:** garbled pitch-factor sentence; waste guidance conflicts; possible waste double-count in cost; one pitch factor for gambrel/mansard; vague OSHA warning.
- **Lesson:** this is the bar — quantities + cost + content + sources at AS 9. We beat it with editable prices, metal vs shingle side-by-side, multi-plane roofs, tear-off, metric and clean maths.

## 5. Patterns

- **Price inputs everyone uses:** roof area (or footprint × pitch factor) · profile · metal · pitch band · tear-off · region · (sometimes) complexity, stories, skylights, dormers, gutters, coating.
- **Quantity maths:** pitch factor √(1 + (rise/12)²); squares = area ÷ 100; panels = roof width along eave ÷ panel coverage width × number of slopes; panel length = run × pitch factor (+ overhang).
- **Price data conflicts** (same product): standing seam steel $5–$18/sq ft depending on page/section; Homewyse $17.74–$23.95. → Must show basis and date; let users edit.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Editable, sourced price table** per profile × metal (only Omni lets you edit one price). Show date and source.
2. **Metal vs asphalt side-by-side on the user's own roof** — upfront cost, lifespan, cost per year (engine-calculated, not hand-typed tables that contradict).
3. **Quantities + cost together:** squares, panels, panel length, ridge/trim linear feet, underlayment rolls, screws/clips (AllRoofingCalc partial).
4. **Multi-plane roofs** (reuse Shingle engine: sections, each with pitch) — no one supports it.
5. **Tear-off layers, stories, skylights, dormers, chimney flashing** as simple add-ons with editable prices.
6. **Metric + currency** (UK/AU metal roofing is common) — 1 competitor.
7. **Gauge and coating** (Galvalume vs galvanised, Kynar/PVDF vs SMP) as price modifiers.
8. **Low-pitch and safety notes** with proper code/manufacturer references.
9. **Engine-generated cost by roof size table** (1,000–3,000 sq ft).
10. **Trust:** date, sources, methodology; no fake reviewers.
