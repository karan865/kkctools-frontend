Outputs: rough total, material and labour subtotals, bundles and nail boxes to buy. PDF, copy, print.

## 5. Patterns

- **Core maths everyone uses:** footprint × pitch multiplier = roof area → ÷ 100 = squares → × 3 = bundles. Multiplier = √(1 + (rise/12)²).
- **Numbers seen (verify from manufacturer sheets before use):** 3 bundles per square for 3-tab (architectural 3–4, premium 4–5 per search snippets); ~33.3 sq ft per bundle; ~29 shingles per 3-tab bundle (varies by brand); 15 lb felt ≈ 400 sq ft/roll, 30 lb ≈ 200 sq ft; ~320 nails per square for 3-tab at 4 nails/shingle; 1 square = 100 sq ft = 9.290304 m².
- **What is always missing:** several roof planes, hip/valley waste, starter strip, ridge cap, drip edge, ice & water shield, shingle-type presets, a sourced bundle-count table. **0 of 6** pages show a date or cite a source.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Full shopping list.** 0 pages calculate starter strip, ridge/hip cap, drip edge, ice & water shield and underlayment together. → Add all with editable coverage per bundle/roll.
2. **Several roof planes.** Nobody lets you add planes (main + porch + dormer). → “Add another section”, each with its own pitch.
3. **Waste by roof type.** Only VibeCalc has waste %, and nobody explains gable vs hip/valley. → Presets (simple gable / hip / complex) + custom.
4. **Shingle type presets.** → 3-tab / architectural / premium with bundles per square and shingles per bundle prefilled (sourced) and editable.
5. **Eaves overhang + pitch from rise/run or angle** (ServiceTitan and Omni have one each).
6. **Bundles-per-square and bundles-by-roof-size tables** generated from our engine (answers the 8,500-search questions directly).
7. **Correct worked examples** — ServiceTitan and VibeCalc both have example errors.
8. **Light cost line** (bundle price + optional labour) linking to Roof Replacement (#19) and Metal Roof (#13).
9. **Metric** (m, m², 9.29 m² per square) for UK/AU users.
10. **Trust:** date + sources + methodology (no page has them).
