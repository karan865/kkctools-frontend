## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### #1 — spicerparts.com engine displacement (AS 41)

- Formula (bore area × stroke × cylinders); example 4" bore, 3.52" stroke, 8 cyl = 353.86 ci. ~150–200 words; intro duplicated; disclaimer “math only”. Calculator didn't render.
- **Check:** with π = 3.1416 the result is ≈ 353.87; with exact π ≈ 353.88 → small rounding slip.
- **Lesson:** ranks #1 with almost nothing. A complete engine mode with presets and FAQ beats it.

### #2 — inchcalculator.com/cubic-inches-calculator/ (AS 81)

- Modes: L × W × H or area × height; inputs mm/cm/m/in/ft/yd (and area units). Output in³. Formula + cm ÷ 16.387 and mm ÷ 16,387 conversions. No example, no FAQ.
- **Lesson:** the **box volume** intent; mixed-unit inputs are useful.

### #8 — powernationtv.com displacement-calculator (AS 40) — **the page to beat**

## 5. Patterns

- **Engine formula:** displacement (in³) = π/4 × bore² × stroke × cylinders = 0.7854 × B² × S × N. 1 in³ = 16.387064 cm³ (exact: 2.54³). 1 L = 1,000 cc = 61.0237 in³.
- **Box formula:** in³ = L × W × H (inches). 1 ft³ = 1,728 in³; 1 US gal = 231 in³; 1 m³ ≈ 61,023.7 in³.
- **Both intents need the same unit converter** (in³ ↔ cc ↔ L ↔ ft³ ↔ gal).
- **3 of 7 pages contain arithmetic or constant errors** (Spicer rounding, Homedit m³ figure, Omni reverse example).
