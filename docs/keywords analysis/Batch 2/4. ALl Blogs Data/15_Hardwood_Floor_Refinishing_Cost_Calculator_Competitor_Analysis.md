- **Title:** Hardwood Floor Refinishing vs Resurfacing (Recoating): Cost, Dust, Time & Best Option

2. **Method selector with plain definitions:** screen & recoat / full sand & refinish / dustless / sand + stain colour change.

4. **Finish options:** coats, oil vs water-based vs hard-wax oil (cost and dry time note).
5. **DIY vs pro:** sander/edger rental per day, sandpaper, stain, finish quantity (coverage from labels), vs pro quote.
6. **Refinish vs replace** side by side (link Laminate Flooring and Carpet Cost pages).
