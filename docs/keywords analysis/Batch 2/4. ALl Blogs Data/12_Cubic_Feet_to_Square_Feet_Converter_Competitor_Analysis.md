## 5. Patterns

- **Maths:** ft³ = ft² × depth(ft) = ft² × depth(in) ÷ 12; ft² = ft³ ÷ depth(ft) = ft³ × 12 ÷ depth(in). yd³ = ft³ ÷ 27. Metric: m³ = m² × depth(m). 1 m³ = 35.3147 ft³; 1 m² = 10.7639 ft².
- **Handy coverage facts (derived, not copied):** 1 ft³ covers 12 ft² at 1 in, 4 ft² at 3 in, 3 ft² at 4 in.
