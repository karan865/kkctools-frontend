### #1 — raumlens.com/cement-calculator (AS 7)

- **Title:** Cement Calculator: Concrete Volume & Bags | RAUMLENS · **H1:** Cement Calculator
- **What the site is:** an AI garden/landscape design site; the calculator is a side tool.
- **Headings:** Your measurements → Material estimate → 01 Choose a volume task → 02 Concrete by yard, cubic foot or cubic meter → 03 Label yield, whole bags and limits → Sources & product specifications → FAQ (3) → More garden material tools.
- **Calculator:**
  - 3 modes: small platform/paving, post holes/fence posts, already-specified footing.
  - Length, width and thickness, each with its own unit menu (ft, in, m, cm, mm).
  - Bag yield is typed in by the user (ft³, yd³, m³ or L) — no bag presets. Waste %, optional price per bag.
  - Outputs: base and waste-adjusted volume in ft³, yd³ and m³; whole bags; optional cost.
  - States “no values are assumed”.
- **Content:** formulas for rectangle, round hole, net post hole (hole minus post), waste and bag count; unit constants; one worked example (10 × 6 ft × 4 in). Cites a QUIKRETE data sheet for bag yield.
- **FAQs:** Is this a concrete or cement calculator? · How do I calculate concrete for fence-post holes? · Can I calculate by yard or for a footing?
- **Weak points:** changing units does **not** convert the numbers already typed; no bag presets; no tables or diagrams; post-hole fields poorly labelled; no author or date.
- **Lesson for us:** the **post volume subtracted from the hole** idea is good and most others miss it. Unit switching must convert values.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **No bags-per-yard table anywhere** (0 of 9), even though “bags in a yard” is the most repeated FAQ. → Add a clear table: 40/50/60/80/90 lb and 20/25/30/40 kg bags per yd³ and per m³.
2. **No metric bag sizes** (0 of 9). → 25 kg and other kg bags for UK/EU/AU users — fits our Europe goal. *Yields must come from manufacturer data sheets; mark `TODO(owner)` if not sourced.*
3. **No page sums different shapes in one job** (0 of 9). → “Add another pour”: slab + footing + 6 post holes → one total.
4. **Formulas for every shape** — most show only the rectangle. → Show formulas for slab, circle, footing, column, Sonotube, post hole (minus post), steps, wall.
5. **Unit switch converts values** — Raumlens doesn't. → Switching ft ↔ m keeps the same physical size.
6. **Ordering logic** — only Numeravo rounds up and counts truck loads. → Round up (0.25 yd³ / 0.5 m³ steps), editable truck capacity, bags vs ready-mix suggestion.
7. **Thickness guide** — only 1 of 9. → Table of typical thickness by project, with a note to follow local code.
8. **Correct numbers** — Concrete-Calc.site has two FAQ errors. → All FAQ answers generated from our engine and unit-tested.
9. **Trust signals** — only Calnovix shows author + date. → Show “last updated” date, sources list, methodology note. *Author info only if the owner provides real details (no fake bios — AGENTS rule 3).*
10. **Diagrams** — only 2 of 9. → Simple inline SVG diagram per shape showing which dimension is which.
