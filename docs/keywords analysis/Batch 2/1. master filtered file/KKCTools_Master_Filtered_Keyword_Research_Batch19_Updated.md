## How to use this master file

- This is a **filtered tool-opportunity list**, not a promise of Google rankings. “DA 1 friendly” means a sensible early target based on the exported metrics and tool intent; it does not mean Top 10 is guaranteed.
- **KD** is Semrush keyword difficulty from the export. **Ad competition** is the `competition_level` field (0–1) and is not organic SEO difficulty.
- **Results** is the exported results count where supplied. It is not a substitute for reviewing the actual Top 10 ranking URLs.
- **12-point trend** preserves the CSV `trend.0` to `trend.11` values in source order. The export does not label the individual months, so do not infer exact seasonal months from this sequence.
- Semrush intent text was mostly blank in this export. Intent notes below are our interpretation of the query wording and must be validated against the live SERP.
- The CSV does **not** contain competitor URL-level authority, page-level referring domains, link quality, or full SERP snapshots. Those checks are required before calling any keyword a genuinely weak-SERP opportunity.

## Important SERP validation before building

For each selected primary keyword, do this in Semrush before committing development time:

1. Open the keyword in the correct country database (this batch is US-only).
2. Inspect all 10 organic ranking URLs, not just the domain-level Authority Score.
3. Record each ranking page’s referring domains, backlink quality, page content and tool usefulness.
4. Look for at least 2–3 genuinely beatable pages: weak/incomplete calculators, thin pages, outdated UX, or pages that do not fully satisfy the query.
5. Confirm that the proposed KKCTools page can match the intent and provide a more useful, accurate tool.
6. Check SERP features and likely click potential; a high-volume query can still produce few clicks when Google answers it directly.
7. Only then mark the keyword “Approved for build”.
