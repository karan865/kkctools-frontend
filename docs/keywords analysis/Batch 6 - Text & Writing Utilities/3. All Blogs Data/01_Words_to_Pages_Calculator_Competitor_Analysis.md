## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. WordCounter.net: Words Per Page (category leader)
- **Inputs:**
  - word count or pasted text
  - fonts: Arial, Calibri, Comic Sans, Courier, Times New Roman, Verdana
  - size 10–14
  - spacing: single, 1.5, double
  - paper: A1–A5 or Letter, in inches or cm
  - 4 margins
- **Tables:**
  - "How many pages is X words" uses 12pt Arial double-spaced, which implies about 274 words per page (1,000 words ≈ 3.7 pages).
  - "Words in X pages" assumes single spacing at 450 words per page.
  - **These two tables don't agree** with each other, or with the 500/250 rule stated on the same page.
- **Weak points:**
  - The H1 is branding, not a description.
  - The default margins are hidden.
  - "Pages: 0" appears twice.
  - Sign-in and Grammarly modal.

### 2. The Write Practice: Words to Pages (book)
- **Inputs:**
  - trim: 5×8, 5.5×8.5, 6×9, 7×10, 8.5×11
  - font 10–14
  - line height 1.2, 1.5 or 1.8
  - margins 0.5, 0.75 or 1 inch
  - words per page (default 250)
- **Weak points:**
  - No visible results, table, examples, FAQ or novel data.
  - Unclear how the options interact with the 250 default.
  - Typo: "it's page count".

### 3. TypeCount: Words to Pages
- **Features:**
  - two directions
  - paste or type
  - presets from 250 to 2,500
  - spacing and size options
- **Outputs:** pages, full pages, remaining words.
- **Table:** 1,000 words = 4 pages, through 20,000 words = 80 pages, all at 250 words per page.
- **Error:** the 500-word row is labelled "One page (double-spaced)" but shows 2.0 pages.
- **Weak points:**
  - Unclear whether the settings change the result ("Based on 250…" is fixed).
  - Says "publishers use 250" without a source.

### 4. Calculate.co.nz: Words to Pages NZ (reviewed Nov 2026)
- **Inputs:** words and spacing (500 / 375 / 250).
- **Outputs:** pages, words per page, and read-aloud time at 140 wpm.
- **Example:** 3,000 words gives 6 pages single-spaced and 12 double-spaced ✔.
- **FAQs (3):** words per page · why spacing matters · whether the result is exact.
- **Weak points:**
  - The 375 figure for 1.5 spacing isn't explained (500 ÷ 1.5 ≈ 333).
  - Rounding isn't stated.
  - No A4 option, even though NZ uses A4.

### 5. CharacterCalculator: Words to Pages
- **Inputs:** 30 fonts, size, spacing. Letter paper; "A4 may vary slightly".
- **Baseline:** 500 words per page in 12pt Arial single-spaced. Examples at 500, 1,000 and 1,500 ✔.
- **Weak points:**
  - Claims an "advanced algorithm" but uses a fixed 500 baseline.
  - No margins.
  - No table or FAQ.

## 5. Patterns

**How to model words per page (our method)**
- Base it on measured characters per line and lines per page for each font, size, paper and margin setting:
  - lines per page = (page height − top − bottom margin) ÷ (font size × line-height factor × spacing)
  - characters per line = (page width − side margins) ÷ (average glyph width × font size)
  - words per page = lines × characters per line ÷ (average word length + 1)
- Calibrate against real documents in Word and Google Docs, and publish the calibration table.
- `TODO(owner)`: approve the calibration set, e.g. 12pt Times New Roman, 1-inch margins, Letter, single spacing ≈ 500–550 words.
- **Always show a range** (±10 %) and the assumptions used.

**Book mode**
- Words per printed page by trim size, e.g. 6×9 at about 250–300 words, labelled as *typical*.
- `TODO(owner)`: find a cited source such as a publisher guide, or present it only as an input the user can change.

**Paragraphs and sentences**
- Defaults: about 15–20 words per sentence and about 100–150 words per paragraph. These must be editable.
- Label them "average assumptions"; they are not rules.

**Essay and novel lengths**
- Novel word counts must come from a cited source, such as a publisher or literary-agent guideline.
- Essay length by level must come from a cited university or style-guide page.
- Never invent these figures (keyword file).

**Table**
- Built by our engine for 100 → 50,000 words.
- Columns: single and double spacing, plus book pages.
