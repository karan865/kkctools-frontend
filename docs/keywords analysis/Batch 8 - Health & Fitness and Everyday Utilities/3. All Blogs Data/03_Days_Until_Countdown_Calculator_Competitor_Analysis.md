## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. Time and Date: Countdown Timer creator (authority)
- **Inputs:** title (with live preview), font, date, time, time zone (city), display format (W/D/H/M/S, D/H/M/S, or days only), stop-at-zero option, about 40 theme presets.
- **Behaviour:** adjusts for DST by location; share via the page URL.
- **Weak points:**
  - About 50 words of content.
  - The midnight default and the "Just Days" option interact in a way that's easy to miss.
  - Duplicate presets.
  - Thumbnails have no alt text.
  - Ads.

### 2. Inch Calculator: Days Until Thanksgiving 2026 (per-holiday page)
- **Shows:** Thu Nov 26, 2026; "12 days" (static) plus a live widget; 1 week 5 days; 288 hours; 8 weekdays; spreadsheet formulas.
- **Errors:**
  - The manual method says to add the days of months "before November". It should be the months *between* now and November.
  - The formulas hard-code 2026.
  - The start-date table includes past dates.
- **Weak points:** no future-year table, no FAQ, no author or date.

### 3. SaturdayGift: Thanksgiving Countdown (Cristina Morero)
- **Shows:** 12 days / 276 h / 16,505 min / 990,241 s, all **static**.
- **Content:** the 4th-Thursday rule, Canada's 2nd Monday of October, and a 2024–2035 table (✔).
- **Weak points:**
  - "Including today" conflicts with how people read "days until".
  - Its federal-holiday link goes to the 2027 page.
  - Mostly navigation.
  - Possible affiliate links.

### 4. Calculator.net: Day Counter
- **Inputs:** start and end dates, include end day, holiday toggle, offset mode, business days only, 14 holiday presets, custom holidays.
- **Content:** a long Doomsday-rule tutorial.
- **Weak points:**
  - Not a countdown.
  - Lists Black Friday and Christmas Eve alongside federal holidays.
  - A variable typo in its algorithm.
  - No FAQ.

### 5. Time and Date: Week Number calculator
- Enter a date or week number to look it up. It shows **no current week or weeks left**.
- The ISO rule appears in the options but is never named.
- Month names mix Hindi and English.

## 5. Patterns

**Engine** (shared with #1 and #2)
- Days until = JDN(target) − JDN(today). Exclusive by default; "include today" is a toggle. State the convention.
- Live countdown: target date/time in the user's time zone. Count down to 00:00 local by default, or to a chosen time.
  - Use `Date.now()` with `setInterval` (1 s) or `requestAnimationFrame`.
  - Pause when the tab is hidden to save battery.

**Holidays computed by rule** (current year and next)
- Thanksgiving (US): 4th Thursday of November.
- Thanksgiving (Canada): 2nd Monday of October.
- Christmas: Dec 25.
- New Year: Jan 1.
- July 4.
- Halloween: Oct 31.
- Valentine's Day: Feb 14.
- Mother's Day (US): 2nd Sunday of May.
- Father's Day (US): 3rd Sunday of June.
- Easter: computed with the Anonymous Gregorian (Meeus/Jones/Butcher) algorithm. `TODO(owner)`: confirm the algorithm source.
- Labor Day, Memorial Day and the other federal holidays (shared list from #1).
- `TODO(owner)`: approve the holiday list.

**Weeks left in the year and ISO week**
- Weeks left = (days remaining in the year) ÷ 7. Show both "X weeks Y days" and the decimal.
- ISO week: the Thursday rule (ISO 8601), named on the page.

**Share link**
- `?to=YYYY-MM-DD&t=HH:MM&tz=…&title=…`
- Sanitise the title text.

**Upcoming common dates**
- List the next 12 months of month starts and popular dates, computed on the client.
- No per-date URLs (keyword-file rule).
