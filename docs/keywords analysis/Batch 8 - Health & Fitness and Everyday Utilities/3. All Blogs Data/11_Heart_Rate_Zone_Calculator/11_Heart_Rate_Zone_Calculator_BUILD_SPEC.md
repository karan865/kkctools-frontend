## 0. Strategy

**5 keywords, ≈ 17,000 searches, all KD 0–12:**
- primary `heart rate zones calculator` **2,900 / KD 0**;
- `heart rate zones by age` 4,400 / KD 12; `how to calculate max heart rate` 4,400; `finding maximum heart rate calculator` 2,900; `zone 2 cardio heart rate` 2,400 (rising).

**Zone 2 is the confusing bit, and that's our angle.** For the same 40-year-old (resting 60), "zone 2" is **108–126 bpm** by % of max, **132–144 bpm** by Karvonen, and **140** by the MAF method: an 18–24 bpm gap. PeakVO2Trainer calls it "5–10 bpm"; Omni's FAQ mixes methods. We show every method side by side and explain why they differ.

**Authority we can cite:** the AHA page (verified 2026-11-22): max HR ≈ 220 − age, moderate 50–70 %, vigorous 70–85 %, resting 60–100 bpm, and its age table, which our engine reproduces exactly.

### Research notes from the competitor analysis

- **Pages read:** Pearson, FatCalc, Omni, PeakVO2Trainer (AHA was 403 then; readable now).
- **FatCalc:** strongest on sources (Fox, Tanaka, Gulati with study details; 8 references; a medical section) but **labels Zone 3 "threshold"** (that's Zone 4), its HRR and %max boundaries don't line up, and it overstates what ACSM says.
- **Pearson:** Fox/Tanaka/known max + Karvonen, 6 FAQs, a good disclaimer, but **zone boundaries aren't shown** and Example 3's "14–15 bpm" holds only for Tanaka (≈ 13 with Fox).
- **Omni:** Karvonen 5-zone model by a PhD author, but **its zone-2 FAQ uses %max**, contradicting the page; the displayed default formula (192 − 0.007 × age²) doesn't match the selector; source list starts at 3.
- **PeakVO2Trainer:** %max + Karvonen, a correct age table, 4 references, but understates the method gap and has no MAF.
- **0 of 4** show all methods side by side with MAF, or a resting-HR how-to with a timer.

## 2. Our edge

- **Max HR three ways + your own:** 220 − age (AHA rule of thumb), Tanaka, Gulati (for women), or a tested max that overrides them; a note that any formula can be off by ±10 bpm or more for an individual (`TODO(owner)`: cite the spread or keep it as a general caution without a number).
- **Zones by %max and by Karvonen side by side,** with the model stated ("common 5-zone model"); bpm ranges, rounded to whole beats.
- **AHA intensity bands** (moderate 50–70 %, vigorous 70–85 %) shown as the cited reference.
- **Zone 2 panel:** %max range, Karvonen range and MAF (labelled as a coaching method with its adjustments), with the gap explained.
- **Age table 20–80** (AHA rule and Tanaka) generated by the engine.
- **Resting-HR how-to with a timer:** count for 30 s × 2, on waking, several days.
- **Safety:** "estimate, not medical advice"; medications (e.g. beta-blockers), pregnancy and heart conditions → "talk to your doctor"; reviewer line.

## 3. Calculator spec

### 3.1 Tabs

1. **My zones** (default)
2. **Zone 2**
3. **By age** (table)
4. **Resting HR** (how-to + timer)

### 3.2 Input guide diagram (above the inputs)

`HeartRateInputGuide.tsx`: a horizontal bpm bar from resting HR to max HR, split into 5 coloured zones (colour + label + number, never colour alone).

- "1 Age → max HR" at the right end; "2 Resting HR" at the left end; "3 Method: % of max / Karvonen" toggle drawn as two stacked bars showing how Karvonen shifts zones up.
- Zone 2 bracket highlighted with its bpm range (live).
- **Zone 2 tab:** three markers (%max, Karvonen, MAF) on one bar with the gap labelled.
- **Resting tab:** a wrist with a 30 s timer icon.
- Live values; the focused field highlights.

### 3.3 Inputs

| Field | Default |
|---|---|
| Age | 40 (15–90; under 18 → note "formulas are for adults") |
| Sex | not set (only used to offer the Gulati formula) |
| Max HR formula | 220 − age (AHA) · Tanaka · Gulati (women) · my tested max |
| Tested max HR | empty |
| Resting HR | 60 (optional; needed for Karvonen; 30–120) |
| Zone method | both columns shown; primary = % of max |
| MAF category | (c) consistent training, no adjustment (a, b, c, d per the MAF page) |

### 3.4 Engine (`src/lib/heart-rate-engine.ts`)

```ts
maxHr: fox = 220 − age ; tanaka = 208 − 0.7·age ; gulati = 206 − 0.88·age ; tested overrides
model (convention, labelled): Z1 50–60, Z2 60–70, Z3 70–80, Z4 80–90, Z5 90–100 (% of max or % of reserve)
zonesPctMax(max) = [lo·max, hi·max] rounded
zonesKarvonen(max, rest) = [(max − rest)·lo + rest, (max − rest)·hi + rest] rounded
ahaBands(max) = moderate [0.5, 0.7]·max, vigorous [0.7, 0.85]·max
maf(age, cat) = 180 − age + {a: −10, b: −5, c: 0, d: +5} ; age ≤ 16 → not applicable
ageTable(20..80 step 5|10) → {age, fox, tanaka, aha 50–85 %, zone2 %max}
```
