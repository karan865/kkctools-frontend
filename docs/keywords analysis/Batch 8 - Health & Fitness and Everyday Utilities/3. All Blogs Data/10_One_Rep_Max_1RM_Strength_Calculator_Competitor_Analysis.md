## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. Strength Level: One Rep Max Calculator
- **Inputs:** lift, kg/lb, reps.
- **Chart:** % of 1RM for 1–30 reps (5 reps = 89 %, 10 = 75 %, 20 = 60 %).
- **Links:** to its own standards pages.
- **Weak points:**
  - Formula undisclosed.
  - No worked example and no RPE.
  - No caveat about accuracy at 30 reps.
  - Thin page that leans on cross-promotion.

### 2. Calculator.net: One Rep Max Calculator
- **Inputs:** weight, reps 1–10, unit, formula (Epley, Brzycki or Lombardi).
- **Checks:** Epley 116.7 ✔ and Brzycki 112.5 ✔ for 100 kg × 5.
- **Content:** definition, how to measure, estimation, training %, tips, safety.
- **Weak points:**
  - Rep-range text contradicts itself.
  - No % table, RPE or FAQ.
  - No author or date.

### 3. GIGAcalculator: 1-Rep Max Calculator (Georgi Georgiev, Jun 2026)
- **Formulas:** 13 available: Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, Adams, Baechle, Berger, Brown, Kemmler, Landers, Mayhew, Naclerio, O'Conner, Wathen.
- **Output:** 1RM plus targets at 50–90 %.
- **Errors:**
  - % chart: 4 reps shows 89 (should be 88), 6 reps shows 84 (83), 12 reps shows 72 (71).
  - Mayhew and Wathen exponents are missing parentheses.
  - Brzycki at 20 reps should be ≈ 47 kg, not 45.
- **Weak points:** 8 of the formulas are undocumented; no RPE; no FAQ.

### 4. Fitness Volt: Epley vs Brzycki (e1RM formula comparison)
- **Formulas:** 7 (Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, Mayhew, O'Conner, Wathen, McGlothin). RPE example: 100 ÷ 0.86 = 116.3 ✔.
- **Errors:**
  - Calls Epley "exact" at 1 rep. It gives 1.033 × the weight.
  - Says Brzycki is "slightly lower at 10 reps". The two are equal there.
  - Says Brzycki fails "above 36". It actually fails at 37.
  - Many claims without sources.
  - Empty "Average" row.

## 5. Patterns

**Formulas (w = weight, r = reps)**

| Formula | Equation |
|---|---|
| Epley | w(1 + r/30) |
| Brzycki | w × 36/(37 − r) |
| Lombardi | w × r^0.10 |
| Mayhew | 100w/(52.2 + 41.9e^(−0.055r)) |
| Wathen | 100w/(48.8 + 53.8e^(−0.075r)) |
| O'Conner | w(1 + 0.025r) |

- Default display: **Epley + Brzycki + Lombardi, plus the average and the min–max spread** (keyword file). Other formulas can be added as options.
- `TODO(owner)`: cite the original publications, e.g. Brzycki 1993 *JOPERD*, Mayhew 1992, Wathen 1994 NSCA, LeSuer 1997 comparison. We cite the papers; we don't copy their tables.

**Accuracy and edge cases**
- Show a note: most accurate at 2–10 reps, less reliable above 10–12.
- r = 1 returns the weight lifted.
- Reject r ≥ 37 for Brzycki.

**% chart**
- 50–100 % in 5 % steps, showing the weight.
- Estimated reps at each % using inverse Epley: r = 30(1/p − 1).

**RPE/RIR table**
- Reps 1–12 × RPE 6–10. RPE 10 means 0 reps in reserve; RPE 6 means 4.
- Derived as %1RM ≈ 1/(1 + (r + RIR)/30). Label it "derived from Epley + RIR", not taken from a proprietary chart.
- `TODO(owner)`: cite the RIR-based RPE concept (e.g. Zourdos/Helms 2016).

**Presets and units**
- Lift presets: bench, squat, deadlift, overhead press. These only change the labels, not the maths.
- kg/lb with plate rounding to 2.5 kg or 5 lb. Link to the Barbell Plate calculator (#32).

**Strength standards**
- Leave them out, unless a dataset with a reuse licence is found. `TODO(owner)`.
- Answer "how strong am I" honestly in the FAQ.
