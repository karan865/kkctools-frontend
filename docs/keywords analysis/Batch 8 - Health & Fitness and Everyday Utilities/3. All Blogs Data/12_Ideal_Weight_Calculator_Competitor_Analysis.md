## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. Calculator.net: Ideal Weight Calculator
- **Inputs:** age (2–80), sex, height in US or metric units.
- **Formulas:** 4. Worked example for a 5'10" man: Devine 73, Robinson 71, Miller 70.3, Hamwi 75 kg ✔. Healthy BMI range 59.9–81 kg ✔.
- **Errors:**
  - The displayed results (72.6 / 71.5 / 75.0 / 77.3) don't match the example.
  - Accepts ages 2–80, but the formulas are for adults, and age is never used.
  - Men's frame-size guidance only covers men over 5'5".
  - Unsourced claim that men weigh "10–20 % more".
- **Weak points:** no chart, FAQ, author or date.

### 2. Inch Calculator: Ideal Body Weight (Holly Smith DO; reviewers Elise Harlow RDN, Donna Schwontkowski DC)
- **Inputs:** sex and height.
- **Formulas:** 4. Examples for a 5'5" woman ✔.
- **Chart:** NIH/WHO BMI chart.
- **Errors:**
  - BMI range given as 18.5–25, 18.5–24.9 and 19–24.9 in different places.
  - The 67″ extreme-obesity bound is duplicated from the 66″ row.
  - The Hamwi result links to a different paper.
  - A duplicated reference.
- **Weak points:** no male or Hamwi example, no disclaimer, no FAQ.

### 3. FatCalc: IBW Calculator
- **Inputs:** sex, units, height, frame (±10 %).
- **Formulas:** 4, with crossover claims ✔.
- **BMI:** 18.5–24.9 (WHO TRS 894). 8 references.
- **Weak points:**
  - No ABW, despite discussing dosing.
  - No example, FAQ, author or date.
  - A couple of historical claims have no source.

### 4. Omni: Ideal Weight Calculator (Szyk, Rain, Zaborowska; reviewer Bowater)
- **Inputs:** sex, height (153–272 cm), weight.
- **Formulas:** 7 (Robinson, Miller, Hamwi, Devine, Broca, Lorentz, Peterson). Example for 183 cm / 80 kg ✔.
- **Disclaimer:** adults only, not for pregnancy, not a replacement for a doctor.
- **FAQs:** 4.
- **Errors:**
  - Says "four formulas" while listing six or seven.
  - Source list starts at 3.
  - Typos.
  - The chart is an image only.

### 5. CDC: Adult BMI Categories (Mar 2024), the authority
- **Categories:** underweight < 18.5; healthy 18.5 to < 25; overweight 25 to < 30; obesity ≥ 30 (classes 1–3).
- **Scope:** adults aged 20 and over. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

## 5. Patterns

**Maths**
- **Formula weights:** base + increment × (height in inches − 60) for each formula and sex. Convert units exactly (1 in = 2.54 cm; 1 kg = 2.20462 lb).
- **Under 5 ft:** show a note that the formulas weren't designed for this. Either still show the result with a warning, or show only the BMI range. `TODO(owner)` / reviewer to decide.
- **Main result:** the range = min–max across the 4 formulas, **next to** the healthy BMI range (18.5 to < 25 × h²) from CDC.
- **Frame:** Hamwi ±10 % (small/large), labelled "traditional adjustment".
- **Optional Peterson 2016:** weight for a target BMI = 2.2 × BMI + 3.5 × BMI × (h − 1.5 m). `TODO(owner)`: cite and include?
- **ABW (dosing):** out of scope. Add a note pointing to a pharmacist or clinician, and give no dosing advice.

**Chart**
- Heights 4'10"–6'6" in 1-inch steps.
- Columns: formula range plus BMI 18.5–24.9 range, for each sex.
- Generated by our code, printable.

**Content**
- Each formula's origin (dosing or diet planning, not a health target).
- BMI limitations (CDC).
- "Talk to a healthcare provider" guidance.
