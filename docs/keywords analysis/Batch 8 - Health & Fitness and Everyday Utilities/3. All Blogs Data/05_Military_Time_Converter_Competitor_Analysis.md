## 5. Patterns

**Rules (shown in full on the page)**
- 12:00 AM (midnight) = 0000.
- 12:xx AM = 00xx.
- 1–11 AM: same hour, 2 digits (0100–1159).
- 12:xx PM (noon) = 12xx.
- 1–11 PM: add 12 to the hour (1300–2359).
- Reverse: if the hour is ≥ 13, subtract 12 **from the hour** and use PM. Hour 12 is PM; hour 00 is 12 AM.
- 2400 means midnight at the end of a day. "2400 Monday" is the same moment as "0000 Tuesday". Some contexts use it; the standard range is 0000–2359.

**Pronunciation**
- 0700: "zero seven hundred (hours)".
- 1430: "fourteen thirty".
- 0005: "zero zero zero five".
- 2000: "twenty hundred".
