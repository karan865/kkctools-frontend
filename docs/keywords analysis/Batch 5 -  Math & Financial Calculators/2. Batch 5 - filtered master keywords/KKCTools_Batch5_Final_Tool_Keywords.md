## How keywords were selected (DA 1 filter)

A keyword made it into the tool list only if **all** of these were true:

1. **KD ≤ 15 for the primary** (up to 25 only when the angle is clearly ours, e.g. construction slopes and right triangles).
2. **Not a giant head term.** Generic heads such as `loan calculator` (2.24M), `mortgage payment calculator` (165,000) or `statistics calculator` (74,000, irregular) are owned by Calculator.net, Bankrate, NerdWallet and similar sites, even when Semrush KD looks low. Semrush KD counts links, not brand strength.
3. **Not heavy YMYL.** Mortgages, loans, retirement and college savings need trust signals a new site does not have.
4. **No live or yearly data needed** (rates, tax tables, admissions data) — the site has zero network calls by design.
5. **No brand or homework-solver intent** (NerdWallet, RogerHub, Photomath, “math help”).

**Rank chance:** ✅ good = exact tool term, KD ≤ 11, no known giant owner · 🟡 possible = KD 12–29 or a known strong site in the SERP; needs links and a better tool · ❌ not now = Section 5.

## What these exports tell us (read first)

- **None of the three seeds is in the files.** `compound interest calculator daily`, `percentage difference calculator` and `cagr calculator online` (and `cagr calculator`) are missing, so their KD is probably > 30. Their close variants are present and used below.
- **Most of the volume is mortgage and loan searches** (about two thirds of the batch). They are **not selected**: low KD, but impossible SERPs for DA 1 (Section 5).
- **Seven rows match tools already planned** in Batch 2 / 3 (percentage change, discount, compound interest, cubic feet). They are added to those plans, not given new pages (Section 3). That avoids two of our own pages competing for one keyword.
- **About 183,820 searches belong to pages that are already live** (gravel, stone, cubic yard, tons). This is the fastest win in the batch (Section 4).
- **Many maths rows are declining.** AI answers and Google’s own boxes are taking simple “X out of Y” and formula searches, so real clicks will be lower than the volumes.

## How to read the tables

- **Role:** Primary = the page’s main target · Secondary – easy = KD ≤ 15 · Secondary – medium = KD 16–24 · Support = KD 25–30, use in H2 / FAQ.
- **Intent** (Semrush codes in the export, decoded as 0 = Info, 1 = Nav, 2 = Comm, 3 = Trans — checked against obvious rows: `rogerhub` = 1, `reverse sales tax calculator` = 3).
- **Trend:** ⚠ irregular = 4+ months at zero (volume unreliable) · ⚠ declining = last 3 months under half of the first 3 · ↑ rising = last 3 months over 1.6× the first 3.
- **Note:** FAQ / H2 = question-type keyword; use it as a heading or FAQ, not as the page target. 🌍 = non-US phrasing.
