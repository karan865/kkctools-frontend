## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. RogerHub: Final Grade Calculator (Roger Chen), the category leader
- **Modes:**
  - basic
  - took the final
  - final counts as a test (test weight, number of tests, test average, how many tests the final counts as)
  - multi-part final (up to 8 parts)
  - point system
  - lowest test dropped (up to 8 tests)
  - advanced (extra credit, makeups, retakes)
- **Formula:** stated, with a PDF formula sheet. The page explains that the final is not included in the current grade.
- **Weak points:**
  - No table of needed scores.
  - No letter grades.
  - No sample output.
  - No date.
  - Dated design, with a Google+ link.

### 2. Omni: Final Grade Calculator (Álvaro Díez; reviewers Czernia PhD and Bowater)
- **Systems:** US Standard, US AP, Canada, GCSE, Australia (schools and university), India CCE.
- **Inputs:** letter, number or % input; current-grade mode or up to 9 prior exams with weights.
- **Example:** 50 % target, 65 % current, final worth 70 % → 43.6 % ✔.
- **FAQs (4):**
  1. How to calculate a final grade
  2. What you need for an A
  3. GCSE pass grade
  4. 15/20 as a %
- **Weak points:**
  - Grade equivalences have no sources.
  - No letter table.
  - Doesn't say whether weights must add up to 100 %.
  - About 2,000 words, much of it filler (anxiety, Steve Jobs).
  - No date.

### 3. CalculatorSoup: Final Grade Calculator (Edward Furey, Oct 2026)
- **Modes:** grade needed on the final · final class grade.
- **Examples:** (90 − 0.6 × 85)/0.4 = 97.5 ✔ · 88.6 × 0.15 + 0.85 × 91 = 90.64 ✔.
- **Weak points:**
  - No warning when the needed score is above 100 %.
  - Only one remaining assessment.
  - No letter scale.
  - SEO filler.
  - No FAQ.

### 4. Pearson: Final Grade Calculator
- **Modes:** required final · predict the course grade · weighted breakdown · what-if planner.
- **Inputs:** letter thresholds and quick targets. It warns when weights don't add up to 100 %.
- **Examples:**
  - Example 1 (86 %, 30 %, 90 %) gives no answer. The correct answer is **99.3 %** ((90 − 60.2)/0.3).
  - Examples 2 and 3 have no numbers.
  - The built-in sample works out to 86.5 %, but the page never says so.
- **FAQs (4):** generic answers.
- **Weak points:** no A/B/C/D table, no author or date, unrelated related links.

### 5. GIGAcalculator: Final Grade Calculator (Georgi Georgiev, Jun 2026)
- **Inputs:** three % fields. Includes a US letter and GPA table (A+ = 4.3, which not every school uses).
- **Weak points:**
  - The formula is printed with mixed units ("100 % − Weight" next to % inputs).
  - "Current grade" is not defined.
  - No example, no edge-case handling, no FAQ.

### 6. RemNote: Final Grade Calculator (study-app lead-gen)
- **Inputs:** current %, final weight, target.
- **Outputs:** the needed score and a table for A/B/C/D, with "secured" when the grade is already reached.
- **Checked with a hidden sample** (88 % current, 30 % final weight):
  - A = 94.7 % ✔
  - B = 61.3 % ✔
  - C = 28 % ✔
- **FAQs (3):** how to calculate · where to find the final's weight · what to do if the needed score is high.
- **Weak points:**
  - The sample inputs behind the table aren't stated.
  - Only one category.
  - The message text is hard-coded.
  - Heavy promotion.
  - No author or date.

## 5. Patterns

- **Core:** needed = (T − C(1 − w)) ÷ w. Reverse: G = C(1 − w) + F·w.
- **Weighted categories:**
  - Course grade = Σ(categoryᵢ × wᵢ) ÷ Σw.
  - With the final as one category: needed = (T − Σ_other(gᵢwᵢ)) ÷ w_final.
  - Normalise the weights when they don't total 100 %, and warn the user.
- **Final counts as N tests:**
  - New test average = (avg × k + F × N) ÷ (k + N).
  - Solve for F.
- **Dropped lowest test:** recompute the test average while excluding the minimum of the scores *including* the final. This may need casework, so solve numerically.
- **Points-based grading:** w_final = final points ÷ total points.
- **Messages:**
  - Needed > 100 %: "not reachable with the final alone; you would need X % even with full marks."
  - Needed ≤ 0 %: "already secured."
  - Optionally show the maximum grade possible (F = 100 %) and the minimum (F = 0 %).
- **Letter table:** needed score for every letter on an editable scale (+/− optional, no gaps between bands). Shares its scale logic with Fraction/Score (#7).
