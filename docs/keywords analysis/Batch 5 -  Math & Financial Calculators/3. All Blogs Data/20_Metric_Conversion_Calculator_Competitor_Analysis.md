**What this tells us:**
- **All-in-one converters are rare.**
  - Omni has 5 categories on one page, with 8–25 units each, including ft-in, yd³ and short tons.
  - Inch has 8 types, but the widget repeats about 8 times.
  - CalculatorSoup and Science Made Simple are **hubs** that link to separate pages.
- **Nobody combines all of these:**
  - **mixed ft + in input with fractional-inch output**
  - **construction units** (yd³, short and metric ton, board feet, sq ft, squares)
  - a **precision control**
  - a **printable chart** for each category
- **Precision is rarely explained.**
  - Calculate.co.nz claims "precise, not rounded" but shows 3.281 ft.
  - Science Made Simple rounds without saying so.
  - Only NIST tells you to use exact factors and round once at the end.
- **The common pitfalls** (US vs UK gallon, fluid ounce vs weight ounce, squaring or cubing factors for area and volume, temperature offsets) are flagged in one search snippet but are not explained well on any of the pages read.

## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### 1. Omni — Conversion Calculator (Rita Rain; reviewer Bogna Szyk)
- **Categories (5):**
  - temperature (8 units)
  - length (21, including ft-in)
  - area (13)
  - volume (25, including yd³)
  - weight (20, including short tons)
- **Examples:** metric prefixes, 1 km² = 10⁶ m², 13 m³ = 1.3 × 10⁷ cm³ ✔.
- **Errors:**
  - "Farhenheit".
  - Says the metre is based on Earth's dimensions. It has been defined via the speed of light since 1983.
  - "neutron test mass".
  - "1 MB = 1,000,000 B" with no note on binary prefixes.
- **Weak points:** no precision setting, no charts, no FAQ, no date, and no temperature example.

### 2. Science Made Simple — Metric Conversion Calculator & Chart
- **Hub of 12 categories:** length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed, pressure, power, time, fuel economy, fractions, metric chart.
- **Factors:** 2.54 cm/in is exact ✔. kg→lb "about 2.205" is rounded without being disclosed (exact: 2.20462).
- **FAQs (6).**
- **Weak points:** calculators sit on separate pages; charts are not visible; no author or date.

### 3. Calculate.co.nz — Metric to Imperial Converter (reviewed Nov 2026)
- **Tabs:** length (mm, cm, m, km), weight (mg, g, kg, t), volume (mL, L, m³), temperature (°C only).
- **Factors:** all correct ✔. Quick-reference table ✔.
- **FAQs (3):** cm → in · kg → lb and stone · °C → °F.
- **Weak points:**
  - Only one direction (metric → imperial).
  - No short ton.
  - US/UK gallon labels are inconsistent.
  - The "precise, not rounded" claim is overstated.

### 4. Inch Calculator — Conversion Calculator (Joe Sexton; reviewer Pateakia Heath PhD)
- **Types (8):** angle, area, length, speed, temperature, time, volume, weight. Includes a factor table of about 22 rows.
- **Weak points:**
  - The widget is duplicated about 8 times.
  - No ft-in combined input and no fractions.
  - No construction units beyond yd³ and sq ft.
  - No FAQ, no date.

### 5. CalculatorSoup — Conversion Calculators hub (Edward Furey, Aug 2025)
- An index of separate converters (25+ topics, including cubic feet/yards, height, cooking, Roman numerals and currency).
- **Weak points:**
  - No all-in-one tool and no sig-fig control.
  - Currency uses an unnamed rates source.
  - Typos ("Coverter", "desnity", "Rankin").

### 6. NIST — Unit Conversion (authority source, updated Feb 2026)
- Points to SP 811 Appendix B (exact factors), HB 44 Appendix C (tables), HB 133, SP 1038 (rounding procedure §4.4) and SP 365 (metric card).
- **Guidance:** use exact factors and round only the final result.
- **Use for us:** cite these as the source of our factors and rounding policy.

## 5. Patterns

- **Exact definitions to build on (verify against SP 811 / HB 44):**
  - 1 in = 2.54 cm
  - 1 ft = 0.3048 m
  - 1 yd = 0.9144 m
  - 1 mi = 1,609.344 m
  - 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg
  - 1 oz (avoirdupois) = 28.349523125 g
  - 1 short ton = 2,000 lb; 1 long ton = 2,240 lb; 1 tonne = 1,000 kg
  - 1 US gal = 231 in³ = 3.785411784 L
  - 1 imperial gal = 4.54609 L
  - 1 US fl oz = 29.5735295625 mL
  - 1 acre = 43,560 ft²
- **Temperature:** °F = °C × 9/5 + 32; K = °C + 273.15. Temperature *differences* use the factor only, with no offset. Explain this.
- **Area and volume:** square or cube the length factor.
- **Fluid ounce vs weight ounce:** they are different quantities. Converting volume to weight needs density, so link to cooking tools (Batch 8 Grams ↔ Cups).
- **Precision:**
  - Compute with exact factors.
  - Let the user pick decimal places or significant figures.
  - Show fractions to 1/2–1/64 for inches.
- **Charts:** generated by our engine for each category (e.g. 1–100 cm → in). Printable.
