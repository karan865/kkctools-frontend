### Features & Controls:
1. **Mode Switcher:** Room Dimensions (L × W × H) vs. Direct Wall Area (gross sq ft + optional ceiling sq ft + doors/windows).
2. **Room Preset Chips:** `10×10`, `12×12`, `12×14`, `14×16`, `16×20`.
3. **Multi-Room Support:** `+ Add another room` (up to 10 rooms), individual room delete, room renaming, and individual ceiling toggles.

   - Smooth Drywall (Standard): 380 sq ft/gal

   - Heavy Texture (Knockdown): 300 sq ft/gal

6. **Price Presets:** Economy ($30) · Mid-Grade ($45) · Premium ($65) · Custom.
7. **Always-Visible Cost Card:** Shows Wall Paint, Ceiling Paint, Primer, Sundries, and Total DIY cost.
8. **Pro Benchmark Card:** Turnkey contractor estimate ($1.00–$2.50 per sq ft of paintable surface) and calculated DIY savings.

## B5. Mathematical Engine Specification

All constants reside in `src/lib/paint-constants.ts` (`LAST_VERIFIED = "2026-11"`).

```
perimeter = 2 × (L + W)
grossWall = perimeter × H
openings  = doors × 21 + windows × 15 + customDeduction
netWall   = max(0, grossWall − openings)
ceiling   = L × W   (only when toggled)
wallGallons    = (totalNetWall × coats) / coverage
ceilingGallons = (totalCeiling × ceilingCoats) / coverage
primerGallons  = ceil((totalNetWall + totalCeiling) × 1 / primerCoverage)
```

### Container Rounding Rule:
For exact gallon value $N$ (with $10^{-9}$ epsilon):
- $\text{frac} == 0 \implies \text{whole gal}$
- $\text{frac} \le 0.25 \implies \text{whole gal} + 1\text{ qt}$
- $\text{frac} \le 0.50 \implies \text{whole gal} + 2\text{ qt}$
- $\text{frac} > 0.50 \implies (\text{whole} + 1)\text{ gal}$

If total purchase $\ge 5$ gallons, shows bucket recommendation hint.

### Cost Formulas:
$$\text{cost}(\text{gal}, \text{qt}, \text{price}) = \text{gal} \times \text{price} + \text{qt} \times \text{price} \times 0.40$$
$$\text{totalDIY} = \text{wallCost} + \text{ceilingCost} + \text{primerCost} + \text{sundries}$$

### PRO Benchmark:
$$\text{paintableSurface} = \text{totalNetWall} + \text{totalCeiling}$$
$$\text{proLow} = \text{round10}(\text{paintableSurface} \times 1.00)$$
$$\text{proHigh} = \text{round10}(\text{paintableSurface} \times 2.50)$$
