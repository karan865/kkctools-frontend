## H2: How Many Yards in a Ton of Gravel?
One US short ton of standard crushed gravel or road base equals approximately 0.71 cubic yards. For rounded river gravel or clean washed stone, one ton equals approximately 0.74 cubic yards. Conversely, one cubic yard of gravel weighs between 1.35 and 1.40 tons.

Because quarries weigh delivery trucks on certified platform scales in tons, while landscaping areas are measured in cubic yards, this 0.71 multiplier is the industry benchmark for estimating driveway gravel and sub-base deliveries.

---

## H2: How to Convert Tons to Cubic Yards (Step-by-Step Formula)
The conversion of tons to cubic yards always needs one extra number, the material density. To convert weight to volume:

1. **Determine Weight in Pounds:** If you have US short tons, multiply by 2,000 (Pounds = Tons × 2,000). For metric tonnes, multiply by 2,204.62.
2. **Find Material Density in Pounds per Cubic Yard:** Every aggregate has a distinct bulk density rating. For example, crushed stone / road base (1.40 tons/yd³) weighs 2,800 lb per cubic yard.
3. **Divide Weight by Density:** Volume (Cubic Yards) = Weight in Pounds ÷ Density in lb/yd³. Alternatively: Volume (Cubic Yards) = Tons ÷ Density in tons/yd³. For example, 14 tons of crushed stone / road base (1.40 tons/yd³): 14 ÷ 1.40 = 10.0 cy.

## H2: Cubic Yards to Tons: How to Convert Volume to Weight
When you measure a garden bed or excavation trench, your dimensions yield cubic yards, but your supplier bills by the ton. When you convert CY to tons:

Tons = Cubic Yards × Density (tons per cubic yard)

For example, if you need 8.5 cubic yards of screened topsoil (density = 1.10 tons/yd³): 8.5 × 1.10 = 9.35 US short tons (18,700 pounds).

## H2: 5 Essential Pro Tips for Buying Bulk Materials by Weight
1. **Beware of Moisture Weight Inflation:** Water weighs 62.4 lb per cubic foot. If a quarry experienced rain over the past 48 hours, stockpiled sand, topsoil, and crusher run absorb substantial water weight. Whenever possible, purchase bulk materials during dry weather periods.
2. **Confirm Truck Scale Calibration:** Legitimate commercial quarries and bulk landscape suppliers use drive-on axle scales certified by your state Department of Weights and Measures. You will receive an official tare-weight ticket showing the truck empty weight, loaded weight, and net material weight.
3. **Know Dump Truck Capacities:**
   - Single-Axle Dump Truck: Carries ~5 to 7 tons (~4 to 5 cubic yards).
   - Tandem-Axle Dump Truck: Carries ~12 to 16 tons (~9 to 12 cubic yards).
   - Tri-Axle Dump Truck: Carries ~18 to 22 tons (~13 to 16 cubic yards).
   *Check your state's legal load limits; real loads are often lower.*
4. **Always Ask for the Split Load Policy:** If you need gravel and topsoil, some suppliers offer split-box trucks that deliver both materials in a single trip for one delivery fee.
5. **Factor in Compaction and Settling:** Use the order buffer in the calculator for settling and compaction (commonly 10-20% for loose gravel and soil). Loose earth materials delivered in a dump truck fluff up with air voids. Once spread and compacted with a plate tamper or lawn roller, loose volume shrinks.

## H2: Frequently Asked Questions
- **How many cubic yards in a ton?** For typical crushed stone or gravel (approx 1.40 tons/yd³), 1 ton equals 0.71 cubic yards. For screened topsoil (approx 1.10 tons/yd³), 1 ton equals 0.91 cubic yards. For lightweight hardwood mulch (approx 0.40 tons/yd³), 1 ton equals 2.50 cubic yards.
- **Is a ton the same as a yard?** No: a ton is a unit of weight (2,000 pounds), while a cubic yard is a unit of volume (27 cubic feet). Converting between the two always requires knowing the specific density of the material you are buying.
- **How many yards in a ton of gravel?** One US short ton of typical crushed gravel or road base equals approximately 0.71 to 0.74 cubic yards. Conversely, one cubic yard of gravel weighs about 1.35 to 1.40 tons (2,700 to 2,800 pounds).
- **How many tons in a cubic yard of sand / topsoil / mulch?** One cubic yard of dry concrete sand weighs 1.35 tons (2,700 lb), wet packed sand weighs 1.60 tons (3,200 lb), screened topsoil weighs 1.10 tons (2,200 lb), and shredded hardwood mulch weighs 0.40 tons (800 lb).
- **Do metric tonnes change the answer?** Yes: one metric tonne equals 1,000 kilograms or 2,204.62 pounds, which is approximately 10.2% heavier than a standard US short ton (2,000 pounds). Our calculator features a dedicated metric unit toggle so you can convert directly from metric tonnes to cubic yards.
- **How many square feet does 1 ton of gravel cover?** 1 ton of gravel covers about 231-240 sq ft at 1 inch, 116-120 sq ft at 2 inches, 77-80 sq ft at 3 inches, 58-60 sq ft at 4 inches and 39-40 sq ft at 6 inches (theoretical, from 1.40 to 1.35 tons/yd3). Add about 10% for compaction and waste. For an exact area x depth quantity use our [stone calculator](/stone-calculator/).
