## B5. Mathematical Calculation Specifications

All calculations are 100% client-side pure math adhering to Zero-Network policy.

### 5.1 Net Floor Area & Perimeter
$$\text{Net Area (sq ft)} = \max\left(0, \sum \text{Add Areas} - \sum \text{Subtract Areas}\right)$$
$$\text{Perimeter (ft)} = \sum_{\text{add areas}} 2 \times (L + W)$$

### 5.2 Gross Order Area
$$\text{Gross Area (sq ft)} = \text{Net Area} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Waste \%}}{100}\right)$$

### 5.3 Float-Safe Box Rounding (Critical Epsilon Fix)
To prevent IEEE-754 float precision errors (e.g., $400 \times 1.10 = 440.00000000000006$ producing 23 boxes instead of 22):
$$\text{Boxes Needed} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Gross Area}}{\text{Box Coverage}} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
$$\text{Purchased Area (sq ft)} = \text{Boxes Needed} \times \text{Box Coverage}$$
$$\text{Surplus Area (sq ft)} = \text{Purchased Area} - \text{Gross Area}$$

### 5.4 Underlayment Rolls (Based on Net Area)
$$\text{Underlayment Rolls} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if attachedPad is true} \\ \left\lceil \frac{\text{Net Area}}{100} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil & \text{if attachedPad is false} \end{cases}$$

### 5.5 Moldings & Trims
$$\text{Molding Pieces (7.8 ft lengths)} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Perimeter} \times 1.10}{7.8} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$

### 5.6 Cost & Benchmarking Formulas (Whole Boxes Bought)
- **Flooring Cost:**
  - Per box basis: $\text{Boxes Needed} \times \text{Box Price}$
  - Per sq ft basis: $\text{Boxes Needed} \times \text{Box Coverage} \times \text{Sq Ft Price}$
- **DIY Total:**
  $$\text{DIY Total} = \text{Flooring Cost} + \text{Underlayment Cost} + \text{Molding Cost} + \text{Sales Tax}$$
- **Professional Contractor Labor Benchmark:**
  - Low: $\text{Net Area} \times \$2.00$
  - High: $\text{Net Area} \times \$5.00$
  $$\text{Estimated DIY Savings} = \text{Pro Labor Range}$$
