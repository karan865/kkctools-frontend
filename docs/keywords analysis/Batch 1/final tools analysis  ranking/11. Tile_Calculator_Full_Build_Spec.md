## B5. Mathematical Calculation Specifications

All math is 100% client-side pure calculation adhering to Zero-Network policy.

### 5.1 Net Surface Area
$$\text{Net Area (sq ft)} = \max\left(0, \sum \text{Add Areas} - \sum \text{Subtract Areas}\right)$$

### 5.2 Single Tile Area & Coverage
Given tile length ($L$) and width ($W$) in inches:
$$\text{Single Tile Area (sq in)} = L \times W$$
$$\text{Single Tile Area (sq ft)} = \frac{L \times W}{144}$$
$$\text{Tiles per Sq Ft} = \frac{1}{\text{Single Tile Area (sq ft)}}$$

### 5.3 Tile Quantities (Single Rounding with Epsilon Guard)
$$\text{Base Tiles} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Net Area} \times 144}{L \times W} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
$$\text{Total Tiles Needed} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Net Area} \times 144 \times (1 + \text{Waste} / 100)}{L \times W} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
$$\text{Waste Tiles} = \text{Total Tiles Needed} - \text{Base Tiles}$$
$$\text{Gross Area (sq ft)} = \text{Net Area} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Waste}}{100}\right)$$

### 5.4 Box Packaging Calculation
- **Tiles per Box Mode:**
  $$\text{Boxes Needed} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Total Tiles Needed}}{\text{Tiles per Box}} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
  $$\text{Total Tiles in Boxes} = \text{Boxes Needed} \times \text{Tiles per Box}$$
  $$\text{Surplus Tiles} = \text{Total Tiles in Boxes} - \text{Total Tiles Needed}$$
- **Sq Ft per Box Mode:**
  $$\text{Boxes Needed} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Gross Area}}{\text{Sq Ft per Box}} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$

### 5.5 Mortar & Grout Estimations
- **Thin-Set Mortar (50 lb bag):** Based on the longest tile side using Net Area:
  - $\le 8" \implies 80\text{ sq ft/bag}$ (1/4" × 1/4" square-notch trowel)
  - $> 8"\text{ to } 15" \implies 60\text{ sq ft/bag}$ (1/4" × 3/8" square-notch trowel)
  - $> 15" \implies 50\text{ sq ft/bag}$ (1/2" × 1/2" square-notch trowel + back-buttering)
  $$\text{Mortar Bags} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Net Area}}{\text{Coverage by Size}} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
- **Sanded Grout (25 lb bag, density 100 lb/ft³, +10% standard overage):**
  $$\text{Grout Weight (lb)} = \text{Net Area} \times \left(\frac{L + W}{L \times W}\right) \times \text{Joint Width (in)} \times \text{Thickness (in)} \times 8.3333 \times 1.10$$
  $$\text{Grout Bags (25 lb)} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Grout Weight (lb)}}{25} - 10^{-9} \right\rceil$$
  *(For mosaic sheets, $L$ and $W$ represent the individual mosaic piece dimensions, e.g. 2" × 2".)*

### 5.6 Project Cost & Contractor Benchmarking
- **Tile Material Cost:**
  - Per sq ft: $\text{Boxes} \times \text{Box Coverage} \times \text{Price}$
  - Per tile: $\text{Boxes} \times \text{Tiles per Box} \times \text{Price}$
  - Per box: $\text{Boxes} \times \text{Price}$
- **DIY Total:**
  $$\text{DIY Total} = \text{Tile Cost} + (\text{Mortar Bags} \times \text{Mortar Price}) + (\text{Grout Bags} \times \text{Grout Price}) + \text{Tools Allowance}$$
- **Professional Contractor Benchmark:**
  - Standard baseline labor: Low = $\$5/\text{sq ft}$, High = $\$15/\text{sq ft}$ of Net Area.
  - Pattern surge: +20% for Diagonal or Herringbone/Chevron ($6 to $18/sq ft).
  - Contractor figures rounded to the nearest $10.
  $$\text{Pro All-In Cost} = \text{Pro Labor} + \text{Materials Cost (Tools Excluded)}$$
  $$\text{Estimated DIY Savings} = \max(0, \text{Pro Labor} - \text{Tools Allowance})$$
