## 30.6 SERP observations and competition assessment

### Positive signals

1. **Very low primary keyword difficulty:** KD is only 14%.

2. **Zero-authority pages ranking on Page 1:** Just like the fence stain SERP, domains with AS 0 are ranking highly. `stainandsealsupply.com` is at #3, and `wooddefender.com` is at #8 with literally zero backlinks or referring domains.

3. **Combined Intent matches Fence Stain:** The core logic required for this calculator is virtually identical to keyword #29.

### Challenges / cautions

1. **Omni Calculator is present:** Unlike the fence SERP, Omni Calculator has a dedicated page ranking at position 2, meaning you have direct, high-quality calculator competition.

2. **Major Paint Brands:** Cabot, Pittsburgh Paints, and Olympic are all on page one.

3. **Social Media in SERP:** The presence of a YouTube video and an Instagram profile suggests Google is scrambling slightly to fill the bottom of page one, which often means opportunity for a targeted, well-built tool.

## 30.7 Opportunity assessment for KKCTools (DA 1)

**Initial assessment: Excellent opportunity. Combine or pair with the Fence Stain Calculator.**

Because `deck stain calculator` and `fence stain calculator` share the exact same major competitors (Cabot, Wood Defender, Stain & Seal Supply, Homewyse) and require the same fundamental math (Square Footage ÷ Coverage Rate = Gallons), this is a highly efficient build. You can either build one master "Wood Stain Calculator" with toggles for "Deck" or "Fence", or clone the codebase to launch two highly targeted, distinct URLs to capture both SERPs independently.

## 30.8 Suggested tool scope

Build an interactive **Deck Stain Calculator** that handles surface area, material quantity, and estimated costs.

### Suggested inputs

* **Deck Floor Dimensions:** Length & Width (feet/meters).

* **Railing Dimensions:** Total length of railing and height.

* **Steps/Stairs:** Number of steps, tread width, and tread depth.

* **Stain Coverage Rate:** Sq Ft per Gallon (default to ~150-250 sq ft).

* **Number of Coats:** 1 or 2.

* **Cost per Gallon:** (Optional).

* **Labor Cost:** (Optional toggle: DIY vs. Pro).

### Suggested outputs

* Total square footage (floor + railings + steps).

* Total gallons of deck stain required (rounded up).

* Estimated material cost.

* Total project cost estimate.
