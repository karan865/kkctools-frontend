## Selection rules
| Factor | Rule |
|---|---|
| Keyword difficulty | Prefer KD 0–29; low KD alone is not enough. |
| Search intent | Must show clear calculator, estimator, converter, or practical tool intent for a standalone tool. |
| Demand & monetization | Consider US volume, CPC, and competition together. |
| Duplicate control | Merge close variants and overlapping intents; avoid duplicate tools and keyword cannibalization. |
| Supporting terms | Keep related long-tail terms under the relevant tool instead of creating thin standalone pages. |
| Validation | All candidates remain provisional until live SERP review confirms intent and competition. |
