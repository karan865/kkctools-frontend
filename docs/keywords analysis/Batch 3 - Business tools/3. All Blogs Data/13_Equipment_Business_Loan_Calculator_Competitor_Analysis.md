## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### NerdWallet — equipment loan & lease calculator — **benchmark**
- **Loan:** price, down payment, term, APR, upfront costs, expected monthly revenue/savings, monthly operating cost → monthly payment, amount financed, total interest, total payments, total cost, **net cash generated**, cash remaining after payment, schedule.
- **Lease:** monthly payment, term, upfront payment, other fees, end-of-lease purchase price → total lease payments, upfront costs, total lease cost, total cost if you buy.
- Content: how much do you need; what is an equipment loan; should you lease; where to get loans.
- **FAQs (3):** best way to use · what APR/term to use · financing vs leasing.
- **Weak points:** no formula or worked example; loan and lease not compared side by side; tax/resale excluded; many ad CTAs.

### Quipli — equipment-loan-calculator (rental software)
- Widget not visible; content on loan amount, rates, terms, requirements, **typical payments for construction equipment**, how to estimate qualification (revenue, **DSCR** example).
- **Error:** skid steer $45,000, 15% down, 8.5%, 60 months → page says $747/mo; correct amortised payment ≈ **$785/mo**.
- Inconsistent minimum loan amounts; empty “Heavy Equipment Cost Calculator” heading; ~⅓ of page is product promo.

### United Capital Source — equipment financing calculator (lender)
- One slider (amount) → payments for 12/24/36/48/60 months at a hidden 6% rate.
- **Error:** every term shows **$299,999** for $3M — impossible (12-month at 6% ≈ $258k).
- Content: how loans are calculated (simplified, no formula), total cost ($500k at 8% over 5 years ≈ $609,791 — roughly right), accuracy, how to use.
- **FAQs (9):** approval difficulty · what can I buy · industries · how much can I borrow · finance or lease · how long · down payment · tax-deductible? · other fees.

## 5. Patterns

- **Maths:** financed = price + taxes/fees − down payment − trade-in; payment = standard amortisation; balloon variant: PV of balloon subtracted; lease total = upfront + payments × n + fees (+ buyout).
- **Decision factors:** total cost, cash flow, tax treatment, ownership at end, maintenance, obsolescence.
- **Errors common:** wrong example payments (Quipli), broken outputs (United Capital) → engine-tested numbers are a real edge.
