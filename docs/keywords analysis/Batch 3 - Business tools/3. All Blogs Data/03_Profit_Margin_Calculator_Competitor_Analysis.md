## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Omni — gross-margin (giant)

- Revenue + COGS → gross profit + gross margin. Example: $1,000,000 revenue, $350,000 COGS → 65%. Sections: definition, how to calculate, why it matters.
- **FAQs (4):** how to calculate · margin when gross profit is half of revenue · what a high margin suggests · what factors influence it. Separate Omni pages for net profit margin and operating margin.

### Inch Calculator — margin-calculator (giant)

- Cost, revenue, profit, margin — any two. Outputs gross margin, profit, markup. Content: formulas (gross, net, markup, ROI), types of margin, how to choose, why important, **limitations**. Examples $3,000/$2,000 and a hair salon with opportunity cost.
- **Errors:** formulas without parentheses; markup written two different ways (one wrong: “(revenue ÷ COGS) ÷ COGS”). No FAQ, no date.

### GIGAcalculator — profit-margin (large)

- Cost + one of revenue/profit/margin. Net vs gross depends on what you include in cost. Example $100k cost / $120k revenue = 16.67%.
- **Errors:** calls gross margin “operating/EBIT margin” and says it's before interest/tax — mixes definitions. “What is a good margin” has no numbers.

### Zendesk — profit-margin-calculator (CRM brand)

- Calculator inputs revenue, cost, markup, profit → margin (gross only; markup and profit inputs unclear). Content: gross, operating, net formulas with examples (40% / 30% / 20%); terms; **6 ways to increase margin**; markup vs margin.
- **FAQs (5):** sales margin vs gross margin · margin vs markup · good profit margin · why it matters · how to calculate 20% margin.
- **Errors:** unclear inputs; unsourced “10% is good”; visible vs schema date conflict; broken share links; empty “customer story” section.

### Aspire — profit-margin-calculator (fintech) — **the page to beat**

- **Calculator:** COGS, selling price (slider), operating expenses, other expenses, margin/markup toggle → **gross, operating and net margin**, gross/operating/net profit, revenue, total cost, markup. Reverse pricing price = COGS ÷ (1 − target).
- Content: what is margin; how to use (3 steps); how it works; markup vs margin; types; **what is a good margin (industry table, 1 source)**; how to improve (raise prices, reduce COGS, cut low-margin products, track spending).
- **FAQs (5):** what is profit margin · how calculated · gross margin formula · sales margin formula · selling price from margin and cost.
- **Errors:** its own calculator example doesn't reproduce (stated 72.2% gross margin; inputs give 78.6%); generalisations unsourced; no author/date.
- **Lesson:** right scope (3 margins). Fix the maths, add per-unit/total, what-if, product mix, currency.

### Xero — margin-calculator (accounting brand, Malaysia page)

- Sales + cost → gross margin. Content: how to use (choose a period), FAQs (what is margin · margin vs markup · formula · why it matters), how to get the margin you want, how to increase margin; markup table **as image only**. Heavy sign-up CTAs; no worked example.

### DQYDJ — gross-profit-margin (finance site)

- Revenue + COGS → gross margin; LaTeX formula; links to separate EBIT, EBITA, EBITDA, operating, net margin calculators. ~250 words. **Error:** says capex is excluded from gross profit (confuses capital spending with costs).
- **Lesson:** “margin family” demand exists (EBITDA etc.) — one combined tool can cover it.

## 5. Patterns

- **Formulas (standard accounting):** gross margin = (revenue − COGS) ÷ revenue · operating margin = (gross profit − operating expenses) ÷ revenue · EBITDA margin = (operating profit + D&A) ÷ revenue · net margin = net income ÷ revenue (after interest + tax) · price for target margin = cost ÷ (1 − g).
- **Common confusions to fix:** margin vs markup; gross vs net; COGS vs operating expenses; margin % vs margin $.
- **Benchmarks:** every page that gives “good margin” numbers is unsourced or single-sourced → if we include benchmarks, cite a primary dataset (e.g., NYU Stern Damodaran margins by industry) or skip.
