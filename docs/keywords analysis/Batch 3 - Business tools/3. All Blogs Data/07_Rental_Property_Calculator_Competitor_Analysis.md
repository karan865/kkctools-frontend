## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Omni — cash-on-cash-return (giant)

- Inputs: total cash invested, gross annual rent (100% occupancy), vacancy %, annual expenses, monthly loan payment → CoC, NOI, annual debt service, annual cash flow. Example $50,000 → 10%. **1 FAQ:** what is a good CoC.
- **Weak points:** example skips the vacancy step; unsourced 8–12%; no date.

### Omni — rental-property (giant)

- Purchase (price, down payment, loan, rate, term), income (rent, vacancy, management fee), expenses (tax, insurance, maintenance, HOA, other), sale (appreciation, holding period, sale price) → loan payment, gross income, cash flow, NOI, CoC, cap rate.
- **FAQs (4):** what is ROI · how to calculate CoC · ROI on a $150k property · good ROI.
- **Errors:** ROI in title but not an output; cash-flow definition omits “other costs”; CoC described as inverted ratio.

### Calculator.net — rental-property (giant)

- Most inputs of any page (purchase, loan, closing, repairs + ARV, 5 expense lines with annual increase, rent, other income, vacancy, management, appreciation, holding length, cost to sell). Content: 50/1/70% rules, IRR, cap rate, CFROI. No examples, no FAQ.

### Landlord Studio — cash-on-cash (landlord SaaS) — **best single tool**

- Inputs: mortgage or all-cash, price, down %, closing, upfront repairs, rate, term, rent, other income, vacancy %, property tax, insurance, maintenance % of rent, **capex reserve %**, management %, HOA, utilities, other. Outputs: CoC, annual cash flow, total cash invested, cap rate, annual figures table.
- Content: how to calculate (3 steps), worked example, what CoC tells you (first-year pre-tax snapshot).
- **FAQs (5):** how to calculate · good CoC · CoC vs cap rate · includes appreciation or tax? · can it be negative?
- **Weak points:** worked example can't be traced; US $ tool with UK/HMRC popups; unsourced benchmarks.

### InvestFourMore — rental-property-cash-return (investor blog)

- Spreadsheet-style calculator (yellow input cells, green results — not readable). Content: why CoC beats ROI for rentals; how to calculate ($25,000 → $5,000 = 20%); what CoC doesn't tell you. Mixed URLs, form residue.

### DoorLoop — cash-on-cash guide (PM SaaS)

- No calculator on page. Formula + **principal-paydown variant** ($5,000 + $500 = 11%); variables; factors (vacancy, surprise expenses, mortgage stage); CoC vs ROI vs cap rate; good CoC; limitations.
- **Errors:** £1,500/month rent shown as $18,000/year; calls cash invested “equity”; FAQ heading empty.

### New Silver — cash-on-cash (lender)

- Loan amount, monthly rent, monthly opex → annual pre-tax cash flow, CoC. **FAQs (3):** does CoC change each year · does cash invested change · does it include appreciation. Broken empty links; no vacancy.

### Wall Street Prep — cash-on-cash (education)

- No web tool; gated Excel. Clear full example: PGI $100k → vacancy $25k → EGI $75k → opex $30k → NOI $45k → debt $20k → cash flow $25k ÷ equity $200k = 12.5%. CoC vs cap rate, vs ROI; good CoC (unsourced).

## 5. Patterns

- **Maths:** NOI = rent + other income − vacancy − operating expenses · annual cash flow = NOI − annual debt service (− capex reserve) · CoC = annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ total cash invested (down + closing + upfront repairs) · cap rate = NOI ÷ price · gross yield = annual rent ÷ price · net yield = NOI ÷ price (UK/AU use) · DSCR = NOI ÷ debt service · total return = cash flow + principal paydown + appreciation.
- **Benchmarks:** “8–12% is good” repeated everywhere with **no source** → avoid stating as fact.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **One dashboard of metrics:** monthly & annual cash flow, CoC, cap rate, gross yield, net yield, DSCR, GRM, break-even occupancy.
2. **Year-1 and 5/10-year view:** rent growth, expense growth, appreciation, principal paydown → total return and IRR (simple, transparent).
3. **Rules-of-thumb checks:** 1% rule, 50% rule — shown as pass/fail with explanation (not advice).
4. **Full cost model:** closing, repairs, capex reserve %, management %, vacancy %, HOA, utilities, insurance, tax.
5. **All-cash vs financed comparison** side by side (shows leverage effect).
6. **UK/AU mode:** “rental yield” wording, £/A$; no US tax assumptions (stay pre-tax).
7. **Traceable worked example** generated by engine (Landlord Studio's isn't).
8. **No sign-up / email gate** (WSP, Landlord Studio push sign-ups).
9. **Benchmarks only with source** — or explain how to set your own target.
