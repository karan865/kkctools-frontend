## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Omni — business-valuation (giant)
- 4 methods: DCF (cash flow, years, discount rate), asset-based, market cap, market multiple (EBITDA/revenue/net profit × multiple). Examples incl. $150,000 × 11.94 EBITDA multiple (dated “at time of writing”, unsourced).
- **FAQs (4):** how much is my business worth · value with $10 stock price · asset-based method · value with $50,000 assets.
- **Weak points:** DCF example not reproducible (terminal value hidden); no SDE; typos; public-company framing.

### Learning Revolution — business-valuation (course/membership businesses)
- Inputs: TTM revenue, opex (excl. owner pay), add-backs (owner draw, one-offs), age, 12-month trend, revenue mix (one-time / mixed / membership), owner dependency. Outputs: range, SDE, multiple range, **valuation at different multiples**, factors panel.
- **FAQs (5):** SDE vs EBITDA · email list value · course business with no team · count affiliate income? · same as Flippa/Empire Flippers?
- **Weak points:** multiple mapping hidden; unsourced 2–4×.

### Website Closers — how to value a small business (broker)
- Guide (no on-page tool; links to their calculator). SDE method (P&L to SDE), EBITDA method (when buyers switch), asset & market approaches, **add-backs and normalising**, value drivers table, calculator vs professional.
- **FAQs (5):** rule of thumb · SDE vs EBITDA · typical multiple · how to value quickly · do I need a formal valuation.
- **Errors:** HVAC example 4.5–5.0× conflicts with own table (3.0–4.5× for that band); multiples unsourced. **AI-prompt manipulation links (see §0).**

### Acquiry — SDE vs EBITDA calculator (M&A advisor) — **best earnings model**
- Inputs: revenue, COGS, opex excl. owner, owner salary & benefits, **replacement manager cost**, personal costs, one-offs, D&A, interest, tax, sector, owner dependence, recurring %. 4 sample profiles. Outputs: value + range, SDE + margin, adjusted EBITDA, reported EBITDA, **earnings bridge**, **pricing by buyer type**, recommended metric, add-back strength.
- **FAQs (8):** SDE vs EBITDA · how to calculate SDE · adjusted EBITDA · when SDE vs EBITDA · SDE multiple for small businesses · EBITDA multiple lower mid-market · accepted add-backs · use for SBA valuation?
- **Errors:** “<$1M SDE uses SDE” vs tool recommending EBITDA at $1.02M; multiples “modelled”, unsourced.

### sum.money — business-valuation (calc site)
- Tabs: Quick (revenue, SDE, industry, age, growth) → low/mid/high; **EBITDA (debt, cash → EV and equity value)**; **Improve value** sliders (margin, recurring %, owner hours). Share URL; embed.
- Example: dental practice $320k SDE at 3.0–4.5× → $960k–$1.44M; EBITDA $140k × 6 = $840k EV − debt + cash = $805k equity.
- **FAQs (5):** how to value · SDE vs EBITDA · three approaches · what increases multiple · how accurate.

### SMBs.com — business-valuation (marketplace)
- Industry, SDE, EBITDA (optional), growth, recurring %, top-customer % → SDE-multiple range. **No method shown**; inputs appear not to change output; heavy CTAs; no FAQ.

## 5. Patterns

- **Formulas:** SDE = net profit + owner salary/benefits + personal (discretionary) expenses + one-off costs + D&A + interest (+ income tax) · EBITDA = net profit + interest + tax + D&A · adjusted EBITDA = SDE − market-rate manager salary · value = earnings × multiple · equity value = EV − debt + cash (± working-capital adjustment).
- **Multiple drivers:** size, growth, recurring revenue, owner dependence, customer concentration, years trading, industry, documentation quality.
- **Rule:** small owner-operated → SDE; larger with management → EBITDA (thresholds vary by source).

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Transparent method:** show every step (earnings bridge → multiple → EV → equity) with the user's numbers; no hidden multiple logic.
2. **User-controlled multiple** with a guided “multiple builder” (base range input + adjustments for growth, recurring, concentration, owner dependence) — explain, don't invent data.
3. **Both SDE and adjusted EBITDA**, with replacement-manager salary.
4. **EV → equity:** debt, cash, working-capital target.
5. **Add-back checklist** with “buyers usually accept / question” notes.
6. **Sensitivity table:** value at multiples ±0.5× and earnings ±10%.
7. **Deal view (simple):** cash at close vs seller note vs earn-out (illustrative; link SBA Loan #12 for buyer financing).
8. **Currency** (UK/EU owners) — 0 competitors.
9. **No lead gate, no AI-prompt tricks**; cited data or none.
