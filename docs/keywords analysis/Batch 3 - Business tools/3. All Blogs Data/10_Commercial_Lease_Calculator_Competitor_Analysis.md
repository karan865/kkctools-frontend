## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Tyler Cauble — rent per square foot guide (broker)
- Calculator: 9 property-type presets, sq ft, base rent $/SF/yr, lease type (NNN, MG, FSG, absolute NNN), NNN/CAM $/SF/yr → monthly rent, annual all-in, base/NNN monthly split, $/SF/month.
- Content: basic math; NNN vs FSG vs MG; how much a landlord should charge (replacement cost × return); **1% rule** and why CRE ignores it; **2026 rent ranges by property type**; tenant mistakes.
- **Errors:** escalation example “$32 at 3% = $36.05 by year five” (4 bumps = $36.02; 5 bumps = $37.10); industrial outdoor storage listed per **month** in an annual table; ranges unsourced; no FAQ.

### Tyler Cauble — commercial lease calculator (broker)
- Sq ft, base rate, NNN/SF → annual and monthly “true rent”. Example 2,000 SF × ($20 + $6) = $52,000/yr ≈ $4,333/mo (correct).
- **FAQs (4):** how is commercial rent calculated · what does NNN mean · gross vs NNN · convert annual to monthly.
- Broken newsletter placeholder; “a NNN” grammar.

### CommercialCafe — how to calculate commercial rent (Yardi portal)
- No calculator. Lease terms (**RSF, USF, load factor** — 1,000 USF × 1.10 = 1,100 RSF); lease types (NNN, modified gross, FSG, **percentage lease** — base + 5% of sales over breakpoint); steps; negotiation.
- **FAQs (6):** what is a load factor · paid monthly or annually · what is rent abatement · are utilities included in NNN · standard annual escalations · RSF vs USF.
- **Error:** modified gross example shows $22 × 1,500 = “$2,750 base rent” without saying it's monthly.

### Calk-USA — commercial-lease (tiny site)
- Base rent, sq ft, lease type (NNN/modified/gross), CAM/NNN, escalation %, term → monthly, annual, total cost, effective rate, 5-year schedule. 5,000 SF retail example.
- **Weak points:** escalation on base + CAM (many leases escalate base only); modified = “half CAM” rule; cites CFPB residential page; FAQs (3) thin.

## 5. Patterns

- **Maths:** annual base = RSF × base rate · annual NNN = RSF × (tax + insurance + CAM) · monthly = annual ÷ 12 · RSF = USF × (1 + load factor) · escalation year n = rate × (1 + e)^(n−1) (base only by default) · total term cost = Σ years − free rent · **net effective rent** = (total rent − free rent − TI value) ÷ (RSF × years).
- **Lease types:** gross / full service gross (landlord pays operating costs) · modified gross (split, varies) · single/double/triple net · absolute NNN · percentage rent (retail).

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Full occupancy cost:** base + taxes + insurance + CAM (separate lines) by lease type, with correct modified-gross handling (user chooses which costs tenant pays).
2. **USF ↔ RSF with load factor** in the tool.
3. **Escalations:** % (base only or all), fixed $/SF bumps; year-by-year schedule.
4. **Free rent, TI allowance → net effective rent** (0 competitors).
5. **Compare two offers** side by side (e.g., $24 NNN vs $32 FSG) → cheapest over the term.
6. **Pro-rata CAM share** = your RSF ÷ building RSF × total CAM.
7. **Percentage rent** mode for retail (breakpoint).
8. **Metric (m²) + currency** (UK/EU leases quote per sq ft or per m² — £/€).
9. **Correct escalation maths** and units (Cauble errors).
