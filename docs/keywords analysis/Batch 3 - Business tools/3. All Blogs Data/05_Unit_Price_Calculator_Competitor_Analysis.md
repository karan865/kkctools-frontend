## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Omni — unit-price (giant)

- Type: weight, volume, items, items with weight, items with volume. Options A and B → unit price each, which is cheaper, you save. Six worked examples incl. **multi-pack soda cans vs 2 L bottle** and **bulk peanuts per lb vs bag per oz**.
- FAQ visible: unit price of a coke in a $6 12-pack. Typos (“#3.00”); 2 products only.

### Inch Calculator — unit-price (giant)

- 6 categories (items, area, length, time, volume, weight), many units; 2 items. One example (32 oz → 2 lb, $6.38 → $3.19/lb). FAQs (3): what is per unit price · how to read it · why use it. Thin content.

### WISEPIM — unit-price (EU PIM SaaS) — most complete tool

- Compare up to **4 products** or **bulk CSV**; EUR/USD/GBP; units g, kg, oz, lb, l, ml, m, items; choose “price per” reference; cheapest marked; **German Grundpreis (PAngV) display** with law references.
- **FAQs (6):** how to calculate · price per ounce · price per ml / 100 ml · compare two package sizes · whole product list · what is the Grundpreis.
- **Weak points:** Germany only for law; sales CTAs; no author.
- **Lesson:** **seller mode** (show legally required unit price) is unique and fits our Europe goal — expand to UK (Price Marking Order) and EU Directive 98/6/EC with sources.

### CalcFYI — unit-price comparator (small)

- Name, price, quantity, unit (oz, lb, g, kg, ml, L, fl oz, gal, ct) for 2 products; output undefined in text. FAQs (3): what is unit price · is larger always better · how stores show unit prices. Vague law claims.

### Calk-USA — unit-price-compare (tiny `pages.dev` site)

- 2 items, price + size/count (no units) → unit price each, better value, savings per unit, savings %. Coffee-bag example. FAQs (3). Cites BLS but uses no data.
- **Lesson:** savings **%** is a nice output, but no unit conversion makes it fragile.

## 5. Patterns

- **Formula:** unit price = price ÷ quantity (in a common unit); per-100 g or per-100 ml for EU-style labels. Conversions: 1 lb = 16 oz = 453.59237 g; 1 oz = 28.349523125 g; 1 US fl oz = 29.5735 ml; 1 US gal = 3.785411784 L.
- **Classic traps:** comparing net weight vs fluid ounces; multi-packs; “bigger is cheaper” not always true; sale price vs regular price; per-sheet/per-roll products (toilet paper).

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Compare 2–6 products** (sizes, brands, stores) in one table, ranked cheapest → dearest.
2. **Multi-pack + per-item size** for every row (6 × 330 ml vs 2 L).
3. **Deals:** coupon $ off, % off, BOGO / “3 for 2”, loyalty price → effective unit price.
4. **Total purchase savings** (“buying 4 of the cheaper option saves $X”), not just per unit.
5. **Choose output unit:** per oz / lb / 100 g / kg / fl oz / 100 ml / L / item / sheet / roll / load.
6. **Seller mode:** required unit price label for UK / EU (per kg, per L, per 100 g) with cited rules.
7. **Shrinkflation check:** old size/price vs new size/price → hidden % price rise (link Percentage Change).
8. **Currency selector**; works offline, no sign-up.
9. **Rich FAQ** (all competitors have ≤ 6 short answers).
