## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Omni — markup (giant)

- 4 linked fields (cost, markup, revenue, profit) — any two solve the rest. Sections: definition, margin vs markup, how to calculate, formula, price management, **markup by industry**, other considerations, 3 book references.
- **FAQs (2):** profit at 40% markup on $50 cost · what does 100% markup mean.
- **Weak points:** formula notation mixes decimal and %; no markup↔margin table; no date.

### Calculator.net — margin-calculator (giant)

- Profit-margin tool shows **margin, profit and markup** together (+ unrelated stock and forex margin tools on the same page). No formulas shown, no FAQ.
- **Lesson:** Google mixes “markup” and “margin” intents — show both outputs.

### CalculatorSoup — markup (giant)

- Inputs: **cost + gross margin %** → markup %, revenue, gross profit. Formulas only. ~300 words. Updated Aug 2025.
- Note the odd design: a “markup” calculator that takes *margin* as input.

### Inch Calculator — markup (giant)

- Cost, revenue, profit, markup (any two). Sections: how to calculate, markup vs gross margin, **cost-plus pricing**, average margin by industry (single secondary source), applying markup, references. Author + PhD reviewer. No FAQ.
- **Error:** markup formula printed without parentheses (“revenue – cost / cost”).

### Sage UK — what-is-markup-percentage (accounting brand)

- Calculator (selling price / cost price / markup % modes). Formulas for markup, margin, selling price, cost price; examples (£100/£60 = 66.67% markup vs 40% margin; fashion 2.5×; burger 50% markup vs 50% margin); **multiple / markup / margin table** (1.25× … 10×); overhead considerations. Author, Sep 2025.
- **Lesson:** the **multiplier (2×, 2.5×, keystone)** view is how retailers think.

### Enerpize — markup (invoicing SaaS)

- 4 modes: cost + markup, cost + price, markup → margin, margin → markup. Content: formulas, what to apply markup to, markup vs margin + 6-row table, **industry table**, **US / UK / Australia sections** (sales tax, VAT, GST — cites IRS, HMRC, ATO), common mistakes, key takeaways.
- **FAQs (6):** markup vs margin · good markup % · calculate manually · **before or after GST/VAT** · **keystone markup** · service businesses.
- **Errors:** grocery markup range differs between table and FAQ; industry table mixes margin and markup in one column; heavy product CTAs.
- **Lesson:** **tax handling by country** is a real question — and matches our Europe/UK goal.

### Stocksmith (ex-Craftybase) — markup (maker SaaS)

- 3 modes: price from markup, markup from price, margin↔markup converters. Content: quick answer, how to use, what is markup, vs margin, formulas, **markup vs margin table at $10 cost**, typical markups by category, **common mistakes**, who should use it.
- **FAQs (6):** what is markup · vs margin · what % should I use · how to calculate % · same as profit margin? · convert margin to markup.
- **Weak points:** single product only; no fees/tax/shipping; unsourced category ranges.

### Calculate.co.nz — markup (NZ)

- Cost + markup → price, profit, markup, margin (live). **GST-exclusive; links to separate GST calculator.** One worked example; 3 FAQs (difference, selling price, markup to margin). Reviewed Nov 2026.

## 5. Patterns

- **Formulas (standard):** price = cost × (1 + m) · markup m = (price − cost) ÷ cost · margin g = (price − cost) ÷ price · g = m ÷ (1 + m) · m = g ÷ (1 − g) · price from margin = cost ÷ (1 − g) · multiplier = price ÷ cost = 1 + m.
- **The #1 confusion:** a 50% markup ≠ 50% margin (it's 33.3%). Every page explains it in text; few make it visible in the tool.
- **Nobody handles real-world price building:** landed cost (shipping, duties, packaging), card/marketplace fees, VAT/GST/sales tax, price endings, wholesale vs retail tiers.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **Price chain:** maker → wholesale → retail with a markup (or multiplier) at each step; shows each party's profit and margin.
2. **Landed cost builder:** product + shipping + packaging + duties + card/marketplace fee % → true cost before markup.
3. **Tax-aware price:** add/strip VAT, GST or sales tax; show price ex- and inc-tax (UK/EU/AU/NZ/US).
4. **Price endings:** round to .99 / .95 / .49 / whole and show the actual markup after rounding.
5. **List mode:** paste a product list (cost per row) → prices at a chosen markup, export/copy.
6. **Always-on margin twin:** every markup result shows margin and multiplier; a live conversion table for common values.
7. **Reverse goals:** “I need 40% margin — what markup / price?”.
8. **Currency selector** (USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, INR).
9. **Plain-language result:** “Selling at $150 gives $50 profit — that's 50% markup and 33.3% margin.”
10. **Trust:** date + method note; link to Profit Margin, Discount, Percentage Change.
