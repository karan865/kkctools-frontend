## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### Inch Calculator — sale-discount (giant)
- Two modes (fixed discount, percent off). Content: how to calculate a discount, convert to %, sale price, **quantity discount**, **sale price table** for $50–$200 × $5–$25 off. FAQs (2): why % matters if I know the $ amount · what is a stackable discount. No reverse, no tax.

### Omni — percentage-discount (giant)
- Price + discount % → price after, saving. Upsell to an advanced page (3-for-2, % off second item, double discount, sales tax). **FAQs (5):** $ amount from % · final price · price before discount · discount from two prices · $120 → $90.

### Calculator.net — discount (giant)
- Percent off or fixed off; **enter any 2 of 4** (before, discount, after, saved). Two examples. No FAQ, no headings.

### DQYDJ — percent-off (finance site)
- Starting price + % → after price, saved. One example ($1,200 laptop at 17%). ~300 words.

### Calculate.co.nz — percent-off (NZ)
- Forward (price + % or preset 5–75%) and reverse (two prices → %). Outputs incl. **common discounts at this price** table and calculation breakdown. Content: how to, reference table, reverse, **percent off vs percentage decrease**. FAQs (3). GST handled on another page.

### LaunchMyStore — discount (ecommerce SaaS) — **feature benchmark**
- Modes: % off, fixed off, **stacked**, **find original**; optional **your cost → margin before/after**; 12 currencies; copy summary.
- **FAQs (8):** how to calculate % discount · how discounts stack · find original price · percent or flat first? · does discount cut margin the same way? · typical ecommerce discount · BOGO/bundle? · tax?
- **Weak points:** no BOGO, no tax; unsourced benchmarks; SaaS CTAs.

## 5. Patterns

- **Formulas:** sale = P × (1 − d) · saved = P × d · d = (P − S) ÷ P · P = S ÷ (1 − d) · stacked: S = P × (1 − d1)(1 − d2) → total = 1 − (1 − d1)(1 − d2) · fixed: S = P − c · with tax: final = S × (1 + t) (tax usually after discount — varies by jurisdiction/coupon type).
- **Margin after discount:** new margin = (S − cost) ÷ S.
