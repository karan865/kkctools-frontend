### Harvest — labor cost calculator for contractors
- Trade selector (general contracting, paving, roofing, decks & fencing, kitchen & bath, electrical, plumbing, painting, custom), labor hours, labor cost/hr, materials, overhead %, profit % → quote price, labor, materials, overhead, total cost, profit, effective billable rate.

- **Weak points:** formula hidden; article says Harvest “doesn't provide a labor cost calculator” under an H1 that is one; heavy promo.

### busybusy — labor burden calculator
- Wage, GL rate per $1,000 payroll, WC rate per $100 payroll; fixed FICA 7.65%, FUTA 6.0%, SUTA 3.0% → each burden line, total burden, total hourly cost (sample $15 → $19.81).
- **Errors:** FUTA shown as 6% of every hour (real effective rate usually 0.6% and only on first $7,000/yr); GL footnote maths doesn't match output; footnote placeholders (“Xx”).
- **Lesson:** the **line-by-line burden breakdown** is what contractors want — done correctly with wage-base caps.

### Omni — labor cost
- Weekly hours, pay rate, absent days, other annual costs → gross hours, gross pay, net hours worked, **annual payroll labor cost, hourly labor cost, labor cost %**. Restaurant example ($10/h, 15 absent days, $3,900 other → $12.60/h, 30.9% of $80k).
- **FAQs (5):** reduce labor costs · fixed or variable · good restaurant labor % · vinyl plank install labor (off-topic) · labor cost per meal.

### Contractor+ — labor calculator

## 5. Patterns

- **Burdened hourly cost** = (annual wages + employer payroll taxes + WC + GL + benefits + paid time off cost) ÷ productive hours.
- **Payroll taxes (US, cite IRS):** Social Security 6.2% up to wage base, Medicare 1.45%, FUTA 6.0% less credit (often 0.6%) on first $7,000, SUTA state rate on state wage base.
- **Job labor cost** = Σ(hours × burdened rate) per role · **quote** = (labor + materials + equipment + subs) × (1 + overhead) × (1 + profit) — or markup vs margin (link Markup #2).
- **Labor %** = labor cost ÷ revenue.
