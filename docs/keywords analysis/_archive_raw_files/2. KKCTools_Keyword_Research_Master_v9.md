## 3. Build recommendations

### Square Footage Calculator
- Inputs: room length and width; allow multiple rooms/areas.
- Outputs: square feet and square metres, total area, and optional waste-adjusted area.
- Target related phrases: room square footage, how to calculate square footage, square feet to square metres.

### Laminate Flooring Calculator
- Inputs: room length/width or direct area; plank/box coverage; waste percentage.
- Outputs: net floor area, adjusted area, number of boxes/packs, and leftover coverage.
- Keep product/brand-specific pack sizes user-editable rather than hard-coded.

### Tile Flooring Cost Estimator – validate before building
- `tile installation cost per square foot` has relevant commercial intent (1,600 US volume; KD 14; CPC $3.12).
- This CSV does not contain an exact `tile flooring calculator` or `tile cost calculator` phrase. Treat this as a possible cost-estimator cluster, not a confirmed standalone calculator opportunity.

## 3. Implementation recommendations
### Pea Gravel Calculator
- Inputs: project length and width (or total area), depth, measurement units, and selected material/bag size where relevant.
- Outputs: cubic feet/cubic yards, estimated tons or weight, and optional number of bags.
- Keep density and bag coverage assumptions visible and editable; gravel weight varies by stone type, moisture, and supplier.

### Fill Dirt Calculator
- Inputs: length, width, depth, and units.
- Outputs: cubic feet, cubic yards, and optional estimated truckloads.
- Clearly label truckload estimates as approximate because truck capacities vary.

### Gravel & Aggregate Calculator
- Consider one flexible calculator with material presets (pea gravel, crushed rock, landscape rock, #57 stone, road base) rather than thin pages for every stone type.
- Add a material-specific density selector for tonnage estimates and explain that local supplier specifications should take precedence.
- `landscape rock calculator` (1,900; KD 13), `crushed rock calculator` (1,300; KD 12), and `river stone calculator` (1,300; KD 12) are useful supporting terms for this cluster.
