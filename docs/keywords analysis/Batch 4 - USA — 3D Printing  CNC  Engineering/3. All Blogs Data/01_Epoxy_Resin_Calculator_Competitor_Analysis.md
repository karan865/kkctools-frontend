## 4. Competitor-by-competitor detail

### TotalBoat — epoxy calculator (brand)
- Flat (L, W, thickness) and round (diameter, thickness) in inches → in³, US fl oz, gallons. Formulas (÷ 1.805 oz, ÷ 128 gal); rule of thumb ≈ 12 sq ft per gallon at 1/8"; add 5–10%; fraction→decimal list; mixing tips.
- **FAQs (4):** is it 100% accurate · actual formula · I know the area — how much? · is a seal coat needed?
- **Weak points:** imperial only; waste not in output; no worked example; kit link only as product grid.

### Omni — epoxy calculator
- Depth, shape (rect/circle), L, W or diameter → volume (oz). Examples 96×48×1 in = 19.95 gal; π×16²×2 = 891.29 fl oz. **FAQs (4):** formula · how much do I need · 24"×24" table · how long epoxy flooring lasts.
- **Errors:** 24"×24" FAQ says 319.7 oz — correct 576 ÷ 1.80469 ≈ **319.2 oz**; “surface of 36 inches” (should be 36 in²); 2-part ratio heading with no ratio maths.

### Jeff Mack Supply — epoxy resin calculator — **tool benchmark**
- Modes: river table (length, narrowest & widest width, depth), rectangle mould, round mould, top coat, void/crack fill, known volume; inches/cm; waste 5/10/15/20% → total litres, Part A/B in litres & grams, **number of pours**, recommended kit.
- **FAQs (3):** epoxy for a river table · measure by weight? · how many pours for deep projects.
- **Weak points:** max-pour depth & density assumptions not disclosed; no example; product CTAs inside tool.

### IntoResin — how much resin do I need (art resin)
- L × W × D → total ÷ 2 (1:1); **water volume method** (fill mold with water, measure; weight × ~1.08 for resin density). Tiny mold example. Imperial only, no FAQ.
- **Lesson:** the **water method** is genuinely useful for irregular art molds.

### EPODEX — epoxy resin calculator
- L, W, “height” (inches) → output not visible. ~300 words, mostly product cards; template placeholder visible.

### MAS Epoxies — epoxy resin volume calculators
- Rectangle & round (inches) → in³, ft³, oz, gal, mL, L; **mix ratio by volume (2:1, 3:1, 5:1) and by weight for 10 named products**. No formulas or FAQ.
- **Lesson:** **ratio by volume vs weight** split is valuable — we offer it generically (user enters ratio and density from their label).

### Woodworking Advisor — epoxy resin calculator — **content benchmark**
- Tool: rectangle only → in³, fl oz, qt, gal, Part A/B at 1:1. Content: what to measure, **depth guide** (seal 1/16", flood 1/8", deep pour up to label max), formulas (rectangle, round, irregular/live edge), conversions, worksheet (waste buffer, edge loss, ratio check), river table, seal coat, topcoat vs sealer.
- **FAQs (6):** how much do I need · calculator · how to figure out · river table · how thick should resin be · should I round up.
- **Errors/weak points:** fixed 1:1 split though article lists 2:1/3:1/5:1; inconsistent edge-loss %; river example not calculated; affiliate products.

## 5. Patterns

- **Maths:** rectangle V = L × W × T · round V = π r² T · river V = L × avg width × depth (or sum of sections) · total = V × (1 + waste + edge) · in³ → fl oz ÷ 1.804688 · → gal ÷ 231 · → mL × 16.387 · parts by volume A = total × a/(a+b) · by weight: mass = volume × density (each part) · pours = ceil(depth ÷ max pour depth) · coverage check: area per gallon = 231 ÷ thickness (in) ÷ 144 sq ft.

## 7. Gaps nobody fills — our opportunities

1. **All shapes in one tool:** rectangle, circle, river (avg width or sections), cylinder/box mold, void fill, known volume, water-method helper.
2. **Several sections summed** (e.g., table top + river + edges).
3. **Coat presets:** seal coat, flood coat, deep pour, with **edge/drip allowance** and waste %.
4. **Generic mix ratio** (any A:B by volume) + **by weight** using densities from the label.
5. **Deep pour planner:** max depth per pour → number of pours, resin per pour.
6. **Kit planner:** best combination of common kit sizes (16 oz, 32 oz, 1 gal, 1.5 gal, 3 gal, 5 gal, 1 L, 5 L) with leftover shown; optional cost per kit → total cost.
7. **Metric and imperial** inputs/outputs together.
8. **Brand-neutral** (no product push).
9. **Correct arithmetic** (Omni FAQ error) and engine-generated examples.
