### 4.2 Paver Sand Calculator

**Problem:** `/sand-calculator/` par bhi "Paver Sand Calculator" H2, do tabs (bedding, polymeric) hain. GSC mein "paver sand calculator" 27.6 par hai, par /paver-sand-calculator/ ki page position 54.6 hai, isliye ho sakta hai Google sand page dikha raha ho (pakka karne ke liye GSC mein page filter lagao). **Owner page: /paver-sand-calculator/.**

**Top 10**

| Pos | Page | Ref domains | Kya hai | Kami |
|:-:|---|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | omnicalculator | 17 | Area, depth, weight, cost, ~700 words | Joint sand aur bags nahi |
| 4 | polymericsandstore | 1 | ~20 products, joint width/depth, ~30 FAQs | Dukaan wala flow |
| 5 | buildvisionai | 3 | Pavers + layers, ~2,000 words | Sand input nahi; base depth 4 in aur 6 in dono likha |
| 6 | alliancegator | 15 | Paver L×W×H + joint → bags, ~150 words | Sirf apne brand |
| 8 | pavercalculator.net | 1 | Bedding + joint, paver size, depth %, bag 40/50/60 | Coverage numbers aapas mein alag |
| 9 | inchcalculator (base) | 28 | Gravel + sand base, size table | Joint sand nahi |
| 10 | blackdiamond | 4 | Joint gap/width/depth → 40 lb bags | Formula nahi |

**Hamari kami**
- Joint sand **fixed table** se aata hai (4 paver styles × 3 widths), jo code mein likhe coverage numbers hain. Competitors paver size + joint width + **joint depth** lete hain.
- Title "Bedding & Polymeric" theek hai, par "how much paver sand" ka chart nahi.
- Copy mein "Industry Standard Code", "universal industry standard" jaise dawe hain.

**Kya karna hai**
1. **Joint sand ka asli formula:** paver length, width, joint width, joint depth.
   - `joint fraction = 1 − (L × W) ÷ ((L + j) × (W + j))`
   - `joint volume = area × joint fraction × joint depth`, fir user ki **bulk density** aur **bag weight** (40/50/60 lb) se bags.
   - Density ka default sirf cited product sheet se. Purane coverage numbers hatao, ya "as printed on product X" source do.
   - Formula rectangular pavers ke liye hai. Flagstone ke liye user apni joint % ya bag coverage daale.
2. **Table (engine se):** 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 500 sq ft × 1 inch bedding → yd³, tons, 50 lb bags.
3. **H2:** "How to calculate paver sand" (880/0), "Polymeric sand calculator" (720/10), "Sand for pavers: bedding vs joint".
4. **Title:** `Paver Sand Calculator – Bedding & Polymeric Sand` (48). **H1:** `Paver Sand Calculator: Bedding and Joint Sand`.
5. "ASTM C33 / industry standard" jaise dawe: source link do ya hatao.
6. `/sand-calculator/` par paver section ko 2 line + link mein chhota karo (Section 5.3).
7. Inlinks 6 hain → 10+: Paver Base, Sand, Cubic Yard, Tons to Cubic Yards, Square Footage, Gravel.
