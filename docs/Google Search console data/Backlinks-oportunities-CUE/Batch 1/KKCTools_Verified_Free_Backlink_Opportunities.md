## Avoid / not counted

- **Paid placement or sponsored posts:** any page that requires a placement fee, sponsored-content fee, paid link insertion, paid review, or paid “featured” slot. Optional paid upgrades are not required for the free routes listed above; do not buy them.
- **Guest-post pages with unclear pricing:** I found live music/home-design/construction guest-post invitations, but their pages did not explicitly confirm that a placement is free. They are **not in the verified count** until the publisher confirms in writing that no fee or reciprocal link is required. Examples checked but not counted: [The Ark of Music](https://thearkofmusic.com/guest-posting/), [Rebel Music Distribution](https://rebelmusicdistribution.com/write-for-us-guest-post-on-rebel-music-distribution/), [ForTheLoveOfBands](https://fortheloveofbands.com/guest-post/), [Musosoup](https://musosoup.com/write-for-us-music), and [Archinomy](https://www.archinomy.com/submit-a-guest-post/).
- **BetaList:** its current submit flow asks the submitter to select a plan and review/pay before publication; not counted as a strict free path. [Submit page](https://betalist.com/submissions/new).
- **Bulk backlink directories/resellers:** lists that promise hundreds of links, guaranteed dofollow, DR/DA boosts, or “we submit you to 1000 sites.” Those are not editorial endorsements and often include irrelevant/low-quality inventory.
- **Reciprocal-link schemes:** no “you link to us and we’ll list you” arrangements. A badge requirement or reciprocal footer link is not acceptable.
- **Irrelevant directories:** casino, crypto, adult, generic local-business, or AI-only directories (KKCTools is not an AI tool). Google Business Profile is not appropriate for an online-only site without eligible in-person customer contact.
- **Question/forum spam:** old Reddit/Quora questions may be archived, and community rules vary. I did not count old threads where open replies or link-permission could not be verified. Do not post a link unless the answer is substantively useful and the community allows it.
- **Conditional vote-to-link launches:** Uneed has a no-cost queue but ties retention/dofollow status to score thresholds and offers paid line-skipping. Do not buy votes or upgrades; treat it only as an optional product-discovery listing, not a backlink target.

## Bottom line

The highest-confidence free routes are **journalist requests (HARO, Featured, Qwoted, SourceBottle)** for the band-name guide, **Hometalk** for a genuinely documented DIY project that cites a calculator, **Product Hunt / Hacker News** for authentic product discovery, and clean brand profiles on LinkedIn/YouTube/Pinterest/X/Facebook/Instagram. Most social and UGC links may be nofollow or otherwise not pass ranking signals; they are included for discovery and brand trust, not guaranteed PageRank.

The 40–60 target was not reached because the verification rules intentionally exclude uncertain fees, paid placements, unverified forums, and spam directories. Expanding the list responsibly requires more direct publisher confirmation and ongoing checks that individual community threads remain open; it should not be solved by relaxing the quality bar.
