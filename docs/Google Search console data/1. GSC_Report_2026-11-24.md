**Website:** `https://www.kkctools.com` (launch 8 Nov 2026, aaj 16 din purani)
**Data:** GSC export `kkctools.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-11-24/` (filter: Last 3 months, Web). Isme **8 se 21 Nov** tak ka data hai. 22–23 Nov ka data GSC me abhi aaya nahi tha.
**Comparison ke liye:** 16 Nov wala export (8–14 Nov ka data, git history me hai).
**Agla kadam:** [2. Top5_SEO_Action_Plan.md](2.%20Top5_SEO_Action_Plan.md)

### Week 1 aur Week 2 ko alag kaise kiya
Dono exports cumulative hain (dono 8 Nov se shuru hote hain). Isliye Week 2 (15–21 Nov) ke numbers maine ghata kar nikale:

- `impressions (week 2) = impressions (24 Nov export) − impressions (16 Nov export)`
- `position (week 2) = (pos₂₄ × impr₂₄ − pos₁₆ × impr₁₆) ÷ impressions (week 2)`

Average position impressions ke hisaab se weighted hoti hai, isliye ye tareeka chalta hai. Ye ek andaza hai, lekin direction sahi batata hai.

> **Data padhne se pehle 4 zaroori baatein**
> 1. **Queries file me exactly 1,000 rows hain.** GSC website se export ki limit 1,000 rows hai. In 1,000 queries ke impressions **2,199** hain, jabki total **4,422** hain. Yaani lagbhag **aadhe impressions** un queries se hain jo file me dikhti hi nahi (privacy ke liye chhupi queries, aur 1,000 ke baad wali queries). Isliye **page-level numbers query-level se zyada bharosemand hain.**
> 2. **Position ka matlab:** Google ki definition ke mutabik ye "the topmost position occupied by a link to your property or page in search results, averaged across all queries" hai ([Google help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828)). **Jis query ka sirf 1 impression hai, uski position ek baar ka snapshot hai,** pakki ranking nahi.
> 3. **Position 50+ par clicks lagbhag nahi aate.** Wahan impressions sirf itna batate hain ki Google ne page ko us query ke liye relevant maana.
> 4. **16 din ka data bahut kam hai.** Nayi site par rankings roz upar-neeche hoti hain (Section 8 me examples hain). Isliye roz ke numbers dekh kar page mat badlo.

## 7. Ek nayi baat: homework queries

Discount aur Unit Price pages par students ke **word problems** aa rahe hain, aur ye **position 6–11** par hain:

- "a portable basketball hoop has an original price of $159. it is on sale for 35% off…" (pos 10)
- "ariana bought two pounds of strawberries for $12.80. what is the price, in dollars per ounce…" (pos 9)
- "at the surf shop a surfboard costs $122. if teddy has a coupon for 15%…" (pos 10)
- "the 30 discount on a $49 dollhouse" (pos 10.4, 7 impressions)

Export me aisi 18 queries hain (31 impressions, weighted position 20.7). **Matlab Google in pages ko "sale price ya unit price ka step-by-step hal" ke liye relevant maan raha hai.** Isliye Discount (16.9) aur Unit Price (13.2) ki page-level position achhi dikh rahi hai, jabki "discount calculator" khud 60 par hai. Isse kaise fayda uthana hai, wo plan ke Section 5 me hai.

## 10. Kya seekha (summary)

1. **Site 16 din purani hai, aur Google pages ko test kar raha hai.** Impressions har hafte badh rahe hain. Ye sahi direction hai.
2. **Target keywords abhi 30–80 par hain, 10–20 par nahi.** 10–20 wali queries zyadatar 1–2 impression ke snapshot hain.
3. **Page 1 ke sabse kareeb ye pages hain:** band-name guide (9.7), genre ideas guide (8.2), Metal (13.9), Boy Band (14.6), Unit Price (13.2), Discount (16.9). Aakhri do ki position homework queries ki wajah se achhi hai.
4. **Sabse zyada demand ye pages dikha rahe hain:** Percentage Change (839 impr), Paint Cost (358), Square Footage (289), Hourly to Salary (211), Linear Foot (208).
5. **Paint Cost par sabse zyada impressions "house painting cost calculator" type queries se hain** (labour + paint ka total). Page ko is intent ke hisaab se check karna hai (plan Section 5).
6. **Kai pages ko abhi ek bhi impression nahi mila.** Page indexing report dekhna hai.
7. **Rankings bahut hil rahi hain.** Kisi page ko 2–3 hafte se pehle dobara mat badlo.
